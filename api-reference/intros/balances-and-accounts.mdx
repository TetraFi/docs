---
title: "Balances and accounts"
description: "Find your venue accounts, read what they hold, deposit into them, and follow credit and fees."
---

1. **List** your venue accounts with `GET …/venue-enablements`. Each account's `accountId` is the `venueAccountId` the other reads take; a wallet-based venue trades from your wallets instead.
2. **Read** balances: every wallet and account at once with `GET …/balances`, or what the venue last reported for one account with `GET …/venue-accounts/{venueAccountId}/balances`.
3. **Deposit** where `…/deposit-instructions` tells you, then **follow** what the venue credited with `…/deposit-history`.
4. **Watch credit** on a credit account with `…/credit` and `…/ledger`, and read the fees you pay with `…/fee-schedule`.
5. **Check** what your workspace may do now, such as request quotes, with `…/capabilities`.

Paths are under `/api/v1/workspaces/{workspaceId}`. None of these reads moves money, and an account that cannot be read is named, never shown as zero. Full explanation: [Balances and fees](/trade/balances).

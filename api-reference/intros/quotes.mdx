---
title: "Quotes"
description: "Ask every venue your workspace can use for a quote in one request, and read a request back."
---

1. **Request** quotes with `POST /api/v1/quotes`. One request covers every venue your workspace can use; filters narrow it, and `venueAsked` says whether any venue was contacted.
2. **Compare** the `quotes`, best first. Every venue considered is in `sources`, with why it did or did not quote.
3. **Order** a quote by its `id` before its `expiresAt`, or requote it to refresh it.
4. **Lost the answer?** Read it back by its `quoteRequestId` with `GET /api/v1/quotes/requests/{quoteRequestId}`.

Full explanation: [Request quotes](/search/how-it-works).

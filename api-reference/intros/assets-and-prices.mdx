---
title: "Assets and prices"
description: "Discover what you can trade and read indicative prices before you ask for a quote."
---

1. **List** the assets your workspace can trade with `GET /api/v1/assets`. Take asset ids and networks from it, never hardcode them.
2. **Read** each venue's indicative price for a pair with `GET /api/v1/prices`. No venue is asked, so it is fast.
3. **Stream** prices for up to 25 pairs with `GET /api/v1/prices/stream` to keep a screen current.
4. **Trade** on a quote, not a price: a price is never firm. [Request quotes](/search/how-it-works) next.

Full explanation: [Prices](/search/prices).

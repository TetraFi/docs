---
title: "Trades and settlement"
description: "Read your trades, and follow how each venue account settles what you and the LP owe each other."
---

1. **List** your trades with `GET …/trades`. A trade on a credit account is not paid at once: it joins that venue account's current **settlement window**, one period with a cut-off and a payment deadline.
2. **Read** the window's statement once its snapshot is ready. It nets the window's trades into **obligations**: what you owe the LP, and what the LP owes you, each with a deadline.
3. **Watch** the deposits and withdrawals on the account. These **movements** are matched to the obligations they pay.
4. **Settle:** when its obligations are met, the window is settled. What is left can carry into a later window.
5. **Plan** ahead with `GET …/settlement/plan`: what is due across all your venue accounts.

Full explanation: [Credit and settlement](/trade/settlement-and-credit).

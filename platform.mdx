One workspace, one compliance policy, one loop: quote, then order. Everything else runs inside the workspace and never reaches your code.

The rule underneath: **a credential acts only within its owner's live permissions**, and trades from an organisation wallet only when that wallet is granted to it.

<Note>
  **Live** is on the production API. **Rolling out** is built and moving through release: [ask for early access](/support). **Coming soon** is in design.
</Note>

| Capability | What it gives you | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Workspaces & personas** | Broker umbrellas with sub-workspaces, companies, LPs, direct users; every call scoped to one workspace, the one an API key belongs to or the one a session names in `X-Workspace-Id`. | Live |
| **[Access & identity](/authentication)** | Custom roles, groups, service accounts. Fine-grained key scopes re-checked against live permissions; request signing (always on for venue-trading keys), OAuth2, MFA. | Live; passkeys rolling out |
| **[Wallets](/core-concepts/wallets-and-signing)** | Organisation and personal wallets, each governed, embedded, self-managed or custodian; several per owner, per-chain defaults. | Rolling out |
| **[Signing without backend keys](/core-concepts/wallets-and-signing)** | Governed wallets with a customer-defined quorum of approvers; bounded delegates the owners approve, with expiry and revocation. | Rolling out |
| **Gas sponsorship** | The gas payer chosen per order. | Coming soon |
| **[Treasury & custody](/core-concepts/treasury)** | Custody connections, vault balances, transfers, whitelists, approval quorums, velocity budgets, statements, in the app; balances and credit readable by API key. | Live; policy and statements rolling out |
| **[Exchange, OTC and fiat](/core-concepts/treasury)** | Desk balances, deposit intents, credit utilisation, an exception queue; bank accounts and ramps as route legs. | Live; fiat rolling out |
| **[Agents](/core-concepts/agents)** | Sponsored service accounts inside a spending envelope, with a kill switch. | Rolling out |
| **[Compliance center](/core-concepts/compliance-policy)** | Pre-trade gate, tighten-only overlays, shadow-mode workflows, sanctions evidence, travel-rule checks, reporting. | Live; workflow builder and reporting rolling out |
| **[Bonded firm quotes](/core-concepts/settlement-smart-contracts)** | A maker's firm commitment is bonded; a proven no-show refunds you. | Rolling out |
| **[Rails and routing](/search/filters-and-sort#chains-and-routes)** | EVM live; Solana, Tron, direct DEX and programmable liquidity, composite routes with recovery. | Live; more rolling out |
| **[Webhooks & notifications](/trade/webhooks)** | An `order.updated` event on every order status change, signed with Standard Webhooks, retried until acknowledged, with a test event. | Live |
| **[Multilateral Netting](/netting-api/introduction), [Shared Collateral Network](/collateral-api/introduction)** | The primitives that retire gross settlement and trapped liquidity. | Coming soon |

One workspace, one compliance policy, one loop: quote, preflight, order. Everything else an institution needs around a trade - who may sign, from which wallet, under whose approval, within what budget - runs inside the workspace, so the signing and integration complexity never reaches your code. The rule the whole platform is built on: **a credential authorizes API access, never wallet authority**.

<Note>
  **Live** is on the production API today. **Rolling out** is built and moving through release - ask us for early access. **Coming soon** is in design.
</Note>

| Capability | What it gives you | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Workspaces & personas** | Broker umbrellas with sub-workspaces, companies, LPs, direct users. Every call is scoped by `X-Workspace-Id`. | Live |
| **[Access & identity](/core-concepts/authentication)** | Custom roles, groups, service accounts, an effective-permissions explainer. API keys carry fine-grained scopes and are re-checked against live permissions on every request. Optional request signing, OAuth2 client credentials, MFA with authenticator apps, passkeys, and recovery codes. | Live; passkeys rolling out |
| **[Wallets](/core-concepts/wallets-and-signing)** | Personal and workspace-owned wallets, several per owner, per-chain defaults. | Rolling out |
| **[Owner-authorized signing](/core-concepts/wallets-and-signing)** | Embedded wallets signed by the owner's own tooling; preflight prepares the exact authorization. | Rolling out |
| **[Session-signer grants](/core-concepts/wallets-and-signing)** | Durable, bounded, owner-approved signing permissions with expiry and revocation. | Rolling out |
| **[Organisation wallets](/core-concepts/wallets-and-signing)** | Member consent and a customer-defined quorum - the co-signer model. | Rolling out |
| **Gas sponsorship** | The gas payer chosen per order and reported by preflight. | Coming soon |
| **[Treasury & custody](/core-concepts/treasury)** | Custody connections, vault balances, transfers, whitelists, approval quorums, velocity budgets, statements. | Live; policy and statements rolling out |
| **[Exchange & OTC desks](/core-concepts/treasury)** | Desk balances, deposit intents, credit utilisation, an exception queue - as settlement lanes. | Live |
| **[Fiat corridors](/core-concepts/treasury)** | Bank accounts and ramp providers per workspace, composed as route legs. | Rolling out |
| **[Agents](/core-concepts/agents)** | Sponsored service accounts inside a spending envelope, with a kill switch. | Rolling out |
| **[Compliance center](/core-concepts/compliance-policy)** | The pre-trade gate, tighten-only workspace overlays, workflows that run in shadow before they enforce, sanctions evidence, travel-rule checks, reporting. | Live; workflow builder and reporting rolling out |
| **[Bonded firm quotes](/core-concepts/settlement-smart-contracts)** | A maker's firm commitment is bonded; a proven no-show refunds you. | Rolling out |
| **[Rails and routing](/router-api/introduction)** | EVM live; Solana and Tron, direct DEX liquidity, programmable liquidity, and composite routes with recovery. | Live; more rolling out |
| **Webhooks & notifications** | Order and settlement events pushed to your endpoint or inbox. | Live |
| **[Multilateral Netting](/netting-api/introduction), [Shared Collateral Network](/collateral-api/introduction)** | The two primitives that retire gross settlement and trapped liquidity. | Coming soon |

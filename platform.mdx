## What is live: Basic execution

You quote every venue your workspace can use in one request, and TetraFi executes your order on your exchange and OTC venue accounts or organisation wallets granted to your key: your assets stay in your accounts.

Advanced execution (TetraFi's router and escrow) is not open yet ([Settlement & Smart Contracts](/core-concepts/settlement-smart-contracts)).

## Coming soon

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Multilateral Netting">
    Your trade price is never touched: a run only decides who pays whom how much, with a validity proof checked on-chain first. You integrate [five touchpoints](/netting-api/integration):

    1. Onboard.
    2. Submit obligations.
    3. Sign once.
    4. Act on each run's notice.
    5. Reconcile the statement.

    Agent platforms add [batches from their own stack](/netting-api/agentic-netting).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Shared Collateral Network">
    A draw is a priced exchange verified in the transaction that makes it. It funds the fill or settlement leg itself and is encumbered only while live. You draw, deposit or curate.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## What the platform handles for you

The workspace decides who may trade, from which account or wallet, under whose approval.

<Note>
  Live: on the production API. Rolling out: built and in release ([ask for early access](/support)). Coming soon: in design.
</Note>

| Capability | Status | What it gives you |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Workspaces & personas | Live | Broker umbrellas with sub-workspaces, companies, LPs, direct users; each call scoped to one workspace (the key's, or the one a session names in `X-Workspace-Id`). |
| [Access & identity](/authentication) | Live; passkeys rolling out | Custom roles, groups, service accounts; key scopes re-checked against live permissions; request signing (always on for venue-trading keys), OAuth2, MFA. |
| [Wallets](/core-concepts/wallets-and-signing) | Rolling out | Organisation and personal wallets: external, hosted or custody; several per owner, per-chain defaults. |
| [Signing without backend keys](/core-concepts/wallets-and-signing) | Rolling out | Hosted wallets with your own group of owners, who approve bounded, expiring, revocable delegates. |
| [Signing from your own wallet](/trade/sign-with-your-own-wallet) | Rolling out | On-chain trades from a wallet only you hold the key to: TetraFi prepares each step, you sign, TetraFi checks and relays. You pay your gas. |
| Gas sponsorship | Coming soon | The gas payer chosen per order. |
| [Treasury & custody](/core-concepts/treasury) | Live; policy and statements rolling out | In the app: provider and custodian connections, vault balances, transfers, whitelists, approval quorums, velocity budgets, statements. By API key: balances and credit. |
| [Exchange, OTC and fiat](/core-concepts/treasury) | Live; fiat rolling out | Desk balances, deposit intents, credit utilisation, an exception queue; bank accounts and ramps as route legs. |
| [Agents](/core-concepts/agents) | Rolling out | Sponsored service accounts inside a spending envelope, with a kill switch. |
| [Compliance center](/core-concepts/compliance-policy) | Live; workflow builder and reporting rolling out | Pre-trade gate, tighten-only overlays, shadow-mode workflows, sanctions evidence, travel-rule checks, reporting. |
| [Bonded firm quotes](/core-concepts/settlement-smart-contracts) | With Advanced, not open yet | A maker's firm commitment is bonded; a proven no-show refunds you. |
| [Rails and routing](/search/how-it-works#filters-and-sort) | Live; more rolling out | EVM live; Solana, Tron, direct DEX and programmable liquidity, composite routes with recovery. |
| [Webhooks & notifications](/trade/webhooks) | Live | `order.updated` on every status change, signed with Standard Webhooks, retried until acknowledged; a test event. |
| [Multilateral Netting](/netting-api/introduction), [Shared Collateral Network](/collateral-api/introduction) | Coming soon | Retire gross settlement and trapped liquidity. |

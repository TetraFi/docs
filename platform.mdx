## What is live: Basic execution

- One request, every venue: a quote request asks every venue your workspace can use and returns comparable quotes, with every venue's outcome.
- Orders on your own accounts: TetraFi executes on your connected exchange and OTC venue accounts, or from organisation wallets granted to your key. Your assets stay in your accounts.
- You integrate: quote, then order, then follow its status or [webhooks](/trade/webhooks).

<Accordion title="Advanced execution (not open yet)">
  With Advanced execution, not open yet, an order settles through your own immutable [router and escrow](/core-concepts/settlement-smart-contracts). Funds release against proof of delivery, or refund through a path nobody can pause.
</Accordion>

## Coming soon

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Multilateral Netting">
    Your trade price is never touched: a run only decides who pays whom how much, with a validity proof checked on-chain first. You integrate [five touchpoints](/netting-api/integration):

    1. Onboard.
    2. Submit obligations.
    3. Sign once.
    4. Act on each run's notice.
    5. Reconcile the statement.

    Agent platforms add [batches from their own stack](/netting-api/agentic-netting).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Shared Collateral Network">
    A draw is a priced exchange verified in the transaction that makes it. It funds the fill or settlement leg itself and is encumbered only while live. You draw, deposit or curate.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## What the platform handles for you

The workspace decides who may trade, from which account or wallet, under whose approval. A credential acts only within its owner's live permissions, and trades from an organisation wallet only when that wallet is granted to it.

| Capability | What it gives you |

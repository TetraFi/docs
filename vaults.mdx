## The Settlement-Time Gap

A token burns in seconds; the value behind it often doesn't. Underlyings liquidate on T+1 cycles or worse - redemption windows, withdrawal queues, out-of-hours cutoffs - so "instant redemption" promises break precisely when they're tested. The emerging market answer is an **instant-liquidity sleeve**: committed capital that fronts the fast leg while the slow leg completes. TetraFi Vaults are that sleeve, built as network infrastructure rather than a per-product patch.

## Two Vault Products

### Instant settlement fronting

Vaults acting as short-term credit lines against T+1 OTC, RWA, and stablecoin **settlement obligations**. TetraFi originates the obligations from integrated LPs, venues, and issuers; solvers draw vault liquidity to settle the trade T+0.

* Trades settle instantly even when the counterparty leg runs on a delayed cycle
* Vault depositors earn the duration spread on short-dated, obligation-backed exposure
* Solvers stop warehousing settlement risk on their own balance sheets

### RWA redemption vaults

Curator-managed vaults whose collateral market makers may use **solely to quote and fill redemptions** through the RFQ flow - turning episodic primary liquidity into continuous secondary liquidity.

* Holders exit tokenised assets instantly, even mid-queue or between redemption windows
* Market makers price duration - bidding for discounted assets against vault capital
* Issuers get dependable secondary liquidity without idle buffers or perpetual incentive spend

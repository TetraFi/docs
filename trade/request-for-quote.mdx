Ask every venue your workspace can use for your exact trade, compare the quotes, and order the one you choose. A desk's firm quote commits to its price until it expires.

Not sure this is the right flow? [Request for quote or Live prices](/trade/rfq-or-live-prices).

## Quote and order

<Steps>
  <Step title="Request quotes">
    Pick **Sell exact** or **Buy exact**, enter the amount and select **Request quotes**. Each row shows **Venue**, **You receive**, **Cost**, **Settles** and **Valid for**; **More filters** narrows the venues.

    <Accordion title="The list filters">
      **All**, **Firm**, **Executable**, **Order book**, **Indicative** and **Needs a step**. A row that needs a step names what is missing before it can be ordered.
    </Accordion>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Order the quote">
    **Choose** a row and check it reads **Quote held**, then select **Execute order**. A row that is not firm yet offers **Get firm quote** or **Execute at the firm price**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Follow it to the end">
    **Trade history** shows it **In progress** until it ends **Completed**, **Failed** or **Refunded**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Firm-quote budgets

Desks limit how many firm quotes they answer, per workspace and UTC day. Ask for a firm quote when you mean to trade, and watch the market on the **Live prices** card.

## Refusals

| The app says | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **Quote expired** | Select **Requote** or **Requote and execute**. Nothing was ordered. |
| **No venue serves** the pair **for this workspace.** | **Connect a counterparty to get prices**, or pick another pair. |
| **Trading not ready** | Finish [Before your first trade](/before-you-trade). |

## Quote and order

<Steps>
  <Step title="Request quotes">
    Name the pair and one amount. Quotes arrive best first; order one whose `next.kind` is `order`. Each states what it commits to in `quality` and until when in `expiresAt` ([Firmness and requotes](/search/firmness-and-lifetime)).

    <QuickstartQuote />

    <Accordion title="Ask desks for firm prices">
      `firmness: "firm"` asks every eligible desk for a firm price up front. With `auto`, the default, a production desk is asked only when you name it in `filters.venues.include`; `indicative` never asks. `filters.firmOnly` then keeps only firm quotes.
    </Accordion>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Order the quote">
    Send its id before `expiresAt`, with `minReceive` when the quote reads `minReceiveRequired: true`. Each quote orders once ([Place an order](/trade/orders)).

    <QuickstartOrder />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Follow it to the end">
    A `2xx` means recorded, not complete: follow `statusUrl` until `terminal` is `true` ([Order status](/trade/order-status)).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Firm-quote budgets

<Accordion title="Each firm ask counts against the venue's day budget">
  Desks limit how many firm quotes they answer. Each firm ask counts against your workspace's budget for that venue, which starts again at 00:00 UTC. B2C2, for example, allows a workspace 200 firm quotes per UTC day.

  <ResponseField name="dayBudget" type="object | null">On a quote: `used`, `limit`, `remaining`, `executorReserve` (the share kept for members who can place orders) and `resetsAt`. Absent when the venue states no limit.</ResponseField>
  <ResponseField name="firmAskAvailableAt" type="string">When the desk takes its next firm ask, when its limit is reached until then.</ResponseField>

  Once the budget is spent, a firm ask answers `429 VENUE_FIRM_QUOTE_LIMITED` or a source with status `firmQuoteLimited`; a member without Place orders is refused `403 DESK_FIRM_RESERVED_FOR_EXECUTORS` once only the reserve is left. Ask only when you mean to trade, and use [prices](/search/prices) to watch the market.
</Accordion>

## Refusals

<Accordion title="Quote order refusals">

| Status and code | Why, and what to do |
| --- | --- |
| `400 MIN_RECEIVE_REQUIRED` | The quote needs your minimum. Set `minReceive`. |
| `409 QUOTE_ALREADY_ORDERED` | The quote was already ordered; `details.orderId` names that order. Read it, do not place it again. |
| `409 VENUE_QUOTE_OUTSIDE_BOUNDS` | The venue's price is below your `minReceive`. Nothing was sent. Act on `details.remediation`. |
| `410 QUOTE_EXPIRED` | Past `expiresAt`. Nothing was sent. [Requote the same id](/search/firmness-and-lifetime#refresh-an-expired-quote). |
| `429 VENUE_FIRM_QUOTE_LIMITED` | The venue's firm-quote budget is spent. Wait `Retry-After`. |

Every refusal: [Place an order](/api-reference/orders/place-order).

</Accordion>

`GET /api/v1/workspaces/{workspaceId}/balances` lists what each of your workspace's wallets and exchange accounts holds, one row per account, asset and chain. It reads the last observation TetraFi holds: it never asks a chain or a venue for a new one, and it never moves money.

<Note>
The API may change until launch. Every change is listed in the [Changelog](/changelog).
</Note>

## Before you start

- Switch on **Treasury read** for the key ([Authentication](/authentication#permissions)). Without it, the read is refused with `403`.
- The path names your workspace: `TETRAFI_WORKSPACE_ID` is the `workspaceId` that `GET /api/v1/me` answers for your key.
- The list holds the accounts the key may read: organisation wallets whose policy lets it read balances, and exchange accounts it may see. Personal wallets are never listed, and bank account balances cannot be observed.

## List the balances

The page holds up to `limit` rows (1 to 100). To read every row, pass `nextCursor` back as `cursor` until it is `null`. A cursor belongs to the filters and the rows it was issued for: when your accounts change while you page, start again without one.

| Field | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `id` | The id of this account, asset and chain. It is opaque: never parse it or build one. |
| `accountKind` | `wallet`, `venueAccount` or `bankAccount`. New kinds may be added: show a row of a kind you do not recognise as it is. |
| `accountId` | A wallet's `walletId`, or the exchange account's `venueAccountId` ([Exchange account balances](/trade/venue-balances)). |
| `asset`, `chain` | The asset, and its CAIP-2 chain. `chain` is `null` for a balance held off-chain, such as at an exchange. |
| `nativeAssetId` | The asset's id at its source, such as a token contract address, when the source names one. |
| `amount` | Everything held, as a decimal string in whole asset units (`"12.5"` is twelve and a half), never base units. |
| `asOf` | When the balance was observed, as an RFC 3339 date-time. |
| `freshness` | `fresh`, `stale` or `partial`. Treat a value you do not recognise as `stale`. |

Narrow the list with query parameters: `asset`, `accountKind` and `accountId`. Any other parameter, or an `accountKind` other than the three above (including the earlier `venue_account` and `bank_account`), answers `400 INVALID_BALANCE_LIST_QUERY`.

## Accounts whose balance is unknown

An account the key may read but whose balance cannot be stated is listed in `unavailable`, never as a row with a zero amount. Show it as unavailable, not as empty. `unavailable` is repeated on every page, and its `reasonCode` says why.

| Field | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `accountKind`, `accountId` | The account, as in the rows. |
| `asset`, `chain` | The asset and chain, when the missing balance is for one of them. |
| `reasonCode` | Why the balance cannot be stated, for example a source that refused the read or an observation with no amount or time. |
| `asOf` | When the source last answered, when it did. |

`incomplete`, when present, means the list may leave accounts out: its `reason` `accessSettingsUnavailable` says the workspace's access settings could not be read, so TetraFi could not tell which accounts the key may see. Read the list again shortly. New reasons may be added: treat any `incomplete` as a list that may be missing accounts.

## Refusals

| `error` | Status | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `INVALID_BALANCE_LIST_QUERY` | 400 | Use only `cursor`, `limit`, `asset`, `accountKind` and `accountId`, with valid values. |
| `INVALID_LIMIT` | 400 | Send a `limit` from 1 to 100, or leave it out. |
| `INVALID_CURSOR` | 400 | The `cursor` was not issued for this list and these filters. Start again without one. |
| `WALLET_ACCESS_CHANGED` | 403 | The key's access changed while the answer was built. Read again. |
| `WORKSPACE_ACCESS_REQUIRED` | 403 | The key's owner is no longer a member of the workspace. Use a current member's key. |
| `VENUE_ACCOUNT_ELIGIBILITY_CHANGED` | 409 | An exchange account was disconnected or became unreadable while the answer was built. Read again: it then appears in `unavailable`. |
| `INVENTORY_UNAVAILABLE`, `VENUE_ACCOUNT_ELIGIBILITY_UNAVAILABLE` | 503 | Nothing was released. Retry shortly. |

Every other code is in [Error codes](/reference/error-codes).

## In the app

**Treasury** shows the same balances, account by account.

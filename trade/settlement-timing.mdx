---
title: "Settlement timing"
description: "Distinguish quote expiry, expected delivery and the deadline recorded on an order."
---

A quote's `expiresAt` is its ordering deadline. Its `eta`, when present, is an expected time to delivery as an ISO 8601 duration. Neither tells you that the order has settled.

## Read the recorded deadline

`GET /api/v1/orders/{orderId}` returns `settlesBy` alongside the public order status:

- For an Advanced order, it is the signed fill deadline or the route's latest committed leg fill deadline.
- For an on-chain Basic swap, it can be the last wallet step's send-by time.
- `null` means no such deadline is recorded. This includes off-chain exchange-account trades and can include same-chain DEX swaps whose calls carry no deadline.

A non-null `settlesBy` is the latest time the order settles **or can no longer execute** under those terms. It is not a guarantee of success or proof that delivery has completed. A null value is not immediate settlement and is not a reason to give up tracking the order.

## While you wait

`filled` means execution happened but settlement or delivery remains. Read the order at `statusUrl` until `terminal` is `true`; do not mark it settled because the quote expired or the clock passed `settlesBy`.

Follow `next.kind: "wait"` after `retryAfter`, or `next.kind: "contact"` by contacting support with `reference`. If the status becomes `outcomeUnknown`, keep tracking the same order: never place a replacement.

[Order status](/trade/order-status) covers polling and recorded fills; [Recover an uncertain order](/trade/recovering-an-order) covers a lost answer. You can also use [webhooks](/trade/webhooks) to learn when to read the order again.

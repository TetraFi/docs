Some desks stream executable prices. Order the one you see directly, without requesting a quote first. The price is executable, not firm: the order fills at or above the least you accept, or not at all.

Not sure this is the right flow? [Request for quote or Live prices](/trade/rfq-or-live-prices).

<View title="In the app" icon="browser">

## Order a Live price

<Steps>
  <Step title="Read Live prices for your size">
    In **Trade**, set **You pay**, **You receive** and the amount. The **Live prices** card shows what a desk streams for that size. A price marked **Indicative** cannot be ordered here: use **Request quotes**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Order the price">
    Select **Trade at the live price**, check the amounts, then **Confirm order**. The price lasts a few seconds, so confirm at once.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Follow it to the end">
    **Trade history** shows the order. It ends filled, or **Not filled** when the price moved past your limit; nothing was traded then.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What TetraFi checks

TetraFi asks the venue for your exact size when you confirm, and sends an order that fills at or above your minimum, or not at all. You never receive less. Each price orders once.

</View>

<View title="With the API" icon="code">

Read a price from [`GET /api/v1/prices`](/api-reference/trading/get-prices) and order it by its `priceId`: one call, no quote request.

## At a glance

| Step | Call |
| --- | --- |
| Read a tradable row | `GET /api/v1/prices?sell=…&buy=…&sizes=…` (Read quotes, `prices_read`) |
| Order it | [`POST /api/v1/orders`](/api-reference/orders/place-order) `{kind: "stream", priceId, slippageBps}` (Place orders, `orders_write`) |
| Follow it | `statusUrl`, as for any order ([Order status](/trade/order-status)) |

## Order a Live price

<Steps>
  <Step title="Read Live prices for your size">
    Pass the amount you sell in `sizes`. A row with `tradable: true` has a `priceId` on each size its venue streams; order it within `validFor` seconds.

    <StreamOrderPrice />

    <Accordion title="Row fields">
      <ResponseField name="tradable" type="boolean">`true` when at least one size carries a `priceId`. Only a venue that streams executable all-in prices to your workspace's own connection is tradable; every other venue is traded through a quote.</ResponseField>
      <ResponseField name="sizes[].priceId" type="string | null">The id that trades exactly this size at this price. It is bound to your workspace.</ResponseField>
      <ResponseField name="validFor" type="integer | null">Whole seconds, at `computedAt`, that the row's ids may still be ordered, a few seconds. Each `priceId` carries its own expiry, which is what the order is judged by.</ResponseField>
      <ResponseField name="notTradableReason" type="string | null">Why a row cannot be ordered: `quoteOnly` (the venue trades through quotes), `notPriced`, `sellAmountOnly`, `sizeNotStreamed` (the venue streams no full price for that size), `priceIdUnavailable` or `priceExpired`. An open set: treat an unknown value as other.</ResponseField>
    </Accordion>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Order the priceId with your limit">
    Send exactly one limit: `slippageBps` (0 to 9999, below the receive amount the price states, rounded up to the asset's precision) or `minReceive` (an exact amount). Keep one `Idempotency-Key` with the body, as for a [quote order](/trade/orders#retries-and-recovery).

    <StreamOrderPlace />

    <Accordion title="Order fields">
      <ParamField body="kind" type="string" required>`stream`.</ParamField>
      <ParamField body="priceId" type="string" required>A tradable row's id for one size, as returned.</ParamField>
      <ParamField body="minReceive" type="string">The least you accept to receive, in units of the asset you buy. Send this or `slippageBps`.</ParamField>
      <ParamField body="slippageBps" type="integer">Your tolerance below the price's receive amount, in basis points.</ParamField>
      <ParamField body="acknowledgeHighSlippage" type="boolean">`true` to accept a limit more than 10% below the venue's receive amount.</ParamField>
      <ParamField body="clientOrderId" type="string">Your reference, 1 to 64 letters, digits, underscores or hyphens.</ParamField>
    </Accordion>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Follow it to the end">
    From here it is an ordinary order of that venue: `operation.placedFrom` reads `stream`. It ends filled, or unfilled with the reason in `operation.dispatch.noFillReason` (for example `priceMoved`) and the remedy in `operation.dispatch.remediation` ([Order status](/trade/order-status)).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What TetraFi checks

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="One order per priceId">
    A `priceId` orders once. Ordering it again answers `409 QUOTE_ALREADY_ORDERED` with `details.orderId` naming the first order: read that order, do not place it again. A retry with the same `Idempotency-Key` and body returns the first answer instead.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Your minimum receive">
    TetraFi asks the venue for your exact size at the moment you order and sends a fill-or-kill order with your minimum as its limit. You never receive less. If the venue's price is already below your minimum, nothing is sent: `409 VENUE_QUOTE_OUTSIDE_BOUNDS` with `details.remediation`, either `widenLimit` (with `details.currentReceive` and `details.requiredSlippageBps`) or `requote`. If an order could not be sent in time, TetraFi tries once more, still inside your limit.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Step-up in a signed-in session">
    API keys are never asked to step up. A signed-in member who turned on the check for orders is refused `403 STEP_UP_REQUIRED` with `details.stepUpSubject`, which names the venue, the pair and the size. The proof, sent in `X-Step-Up-Proof`, covers those terms only: a proof for another size answers `STEP_UP_SUBJECT_MISMATCH`, and a used proof `STEP_UP_ALREADY_USED`.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Refusals

<Accordion title="Live prices order refusals">

| Status and code | Why, and what to do |
| --- | --- |
| `400 PRICE_ID_INVALID` | The `priceId` was altered, or issued to another workspace. Nothing was sent. Read Live prices again and order an id as returned. |
| `400 INVALID_REQUEST` | No limit, both limits, or an unknown field (`details.field`). Send `kind`, `priceId` and one limit. |
| `409 PRICE_EXPIRED` | The id's `validFor` lapsed before the order arrived. Nothing was sent. Read Live prices again and order at once. |
| `409 QUOTE_ALREADY_ORDERED` | The id was already ordered; `details.orderId` names that order. |
| `409 VENUE_QUOTE_OUTSIDE_BOUNDS` | The venue's price is below your minimum. Nothing was sent. Act on `details.remediation`. |
| `409 VENUE_QUOTE_NOT_PRICED` | The venue gave no price for your size; `details.cause` says why, for example `firmQuoteLimited`. Read Live prices again. |
| `422 HIGH_SLIPPAGE_ACK_REQUIRED` | Your limit is more than 10% below the venue's receive amount. Add `acknowledgeHighSlippage: true`, or tighten it. |
| `503 PRICE_ID_UNAVAILABLE` | The id could not be checked right now. Nothing was sent. Retry the same request shortly, with backoff. |

Every other refusal is that of a quote order: [Place an order](/api-reference/orders/place-order).

</Accordion>

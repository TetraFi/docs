A desk venue can be traded two ways. **Request for quote** asks the venue for your exact trade and holds its answer. **Live prices** orders the price the venue is already streaming, in one call. Both end as the same order of that venue, followed the same way.

## At a glance

| | Request for quote | Live prices |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Calls | `POST /api/v1/quotes`, then `POST /api/v1/orders {kind: "quote", quoteId}` | `GET /api/v1/prices`, then `POST /api/v1/orders {kind: "stream", priceId}` |
| Speed | A venue round trip for the quote, then the order | No quote request: the order is the only call that asks the venue |
| Price certainty | A firm quote delivers exactly `buy.amount` until `expiresAt` | Executable, not firm: the price can move between your read and your order |
| Your protection | The quote's terms; `minReceive` where the quote asks for it | Your `minReceive` or `slippageBps`, always: the order fills at or above it, or not at all |
| How long you have | Until the quote's `expiresAt` | The row's `validFor`, a few seconds |
| Firm-quote budget | Each firm ask counts against the venue's day budget | Reads streamed prices, so watching the market spends no firm ask |
| Venues | Every venue | Desks that stream executable prices to your connection (`tradable: true`) |

## Which to use

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Use Request for quote when">
    - you need a price that will not move while you decide, or must show a person the exact amount before they accept;
    - the size is larger than the venue streams, or the row reads `sizeNotStreamed` or `quoteOnly`;
    - you compare every venue, on chain and off, for one trade.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Use Live prices when">
    - you trade often, or quickly, and a few basis points of movement inside your limit are acceptable;
    - you watch a desk's prices continuously and want to act on the one you see;
    - you would otherwise spend firm quotes to watch the market. B2C2, for example, allows a workspace 200 firm quotes per UTC day.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What is the same">
    The same permission (Place orders), `Idempotency-Key` rules, order resource, statuses and refusals of the venue. A stream order is recorded with `operation.placedFrom: "stream"`, and a quote order with `quote`.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

---
title: "Recover an uncertain order"
description: "After a timeout, server error or unknown outcome, find the original order instead of placing a replacement."
---

A timeout, dropped connection or `5xx` answer does not prove that an order failed. Neither does `status: "outcomeUnknown"`: the trade may have filled. Never place a fresh order to make sure.

## Recover in this order

1. **Replay the original request.** Resend `POST /api/v1/orders` with the same body and the same `Idempotency-Key`. A replay returns the original answer without placing another order, even after the quote expires. Keep the key and body with your order record before the first send.
2. **Find what the quote produced.** Call `GET /api/v1/orders/by-quote/{quoteId}` with a key that can read orders in that workspace. This read never places anything and works after quote expiry. Use it directly if you lost the idempotency key.
3. **Follow the recovered order.** When recovery says `state: "ordered"`, read its `statusUrl`. Keep reading until `terminal` is `true`, even if the current status is `outcomeUnknown`.

The response from `GET /api/v1/orders/by-quote/{quoteId}` names the order without requiring you to guess its id:

```json
{"state":"ordered","orderId":"…","statusUrl":"/api/v1/orders/…"}
```

## If recovery cannot decide

| Recovery answer | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| `state: "unknown"` | Wait and repeat the read. This is not permission to submit again. |
| `state: "notOrdered"` | No order was placed with this quote. Check `expired` before proceeding; [Idempotency](/trade/idempotency#recover-an-order-by-its-quote) explains both cases. |
| A failed recovery read | The outcome is still unknown to you. Retry the read, never a replacement order. |

For an existing order, follow `next.kind: "wait"` after its `retryAfter` duration; for `next.kind: "contact"`, contact support with `reference`. The [order-status guide](/trade/order-status#while-it-is-not-final) covers polling, and [Idempotency](/trade/idempotency) lists the retry and conflict responses.

Do not change the body or rotate the idempotency key to escape a timeout or refusal. Only a successful recovery read or a final order outcome can establish what happened.

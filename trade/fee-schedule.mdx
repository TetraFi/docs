`GET /api/v1/workspaces/{workspaceId}/fee-schedule` answers the TetraFi fees your workspace pays: the schedule in force now, and every change already scheduled. It reads only.

<Note>
The API may change until launch. Every change is listed in the [Changelog](/changelog).
</Note>

## Before you start

- Switch on **Read fee schedule** for the key ([Authentication](/authentication#permissions)). Every member of a workspace can read its fees, but a key reads them only when this permission is ticked: no other permission includes it.
- The path names your workspace: `TETRAFI_WORKSPACE_ID` is the `workspaceId` that `GET /api/v1/me` answers for your key.

## Read the schedule

`current` is the schedule in force. `upcoming` lists the changes already scheduled, oldest first, each with the `effectiveFrom` it starts at and the whole `schedule` from then on. `[]` means no change is scheduled.

A schedule has one set of `defaultRates` and, in `venues`, the venues whose rates differ: a venue listed there uses its own `rates`, and every other venue uses `defaultRates`. `partnerArrangementApplies` is `true` when your workspace has a partner arrangement with the venue.

| Rate | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `platform` | The rate you are charged, after any taker incentive. |
| `grossPlatform` | The rate before the taker incentive. |
| `takerIncentive` | The share of the gross fee rebated to your workspace. `null` when there is none. |
| `makerShare` | The share of fees your workspace earns as the venue's partner maker. `null` when there is none. It never shows another workspace's arrangement. |

Each rate has `ratePpm`, a decimal string in parts per million (100 ppm is one basis point, 0.01%), and the `effectiveFrom` it applies from, as an RFC 3339 date-time. Read `ratePpm` as a decimal, not a float.

```json Response (shortened)
{
  "workspaceId": "36ff6481-…",

    "defaultRates": {
      "platform": { "ratePpm": "100", "effectiveFrom": "2026-10-27T00:00:00Z" },
      "grossPlatform": { "ratePpm": "100", "effectiveFrom": "2026-10-27T00:00:00Z" },
      "takerIncentive": null,
      "makerShare": null
    },
    "venues": [],
    "partnerArrangementApplies": false
  },
  "upcoming": []
}
```

## Refusals

| `error` | Status | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `INVALID_REQUEST` | 400 | The path's workspace id is not a UUID. |
| `FEE_SCHEDULE_UNAVAILABLE` | 503 | The schedule could not be read, and no partial or zero schedule was sent. Retry shortly. |

A key without **Read fee schedule** is refused with `403`. Every other code is in [Error codes](/reference/error-codes).

## In the app

The app does not show your workspace's fee schedule yet: read it with this request.

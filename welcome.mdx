Two engines power TetraFi. **Firm RFQ liquidity**: solvers and LPs competing for your flow under escrowed, delivery-or-refund settlement. **Multi-source routing**: every eligible path - DEX, bridge, issuer, fiat - returned as ranked, executable candidates. Underneath both sit immutable contracts your institution deploys and owns, plugged into a shared settlement network: institution-grade execution inside your own regulatory perimeter.

Both are tuned for one segment: [regulated stablecoins and tokenized real-world assets](/why-tetrafi), at institutional ticket sizes, between identified counterparties - covering secondary trading, primary issuance, and redemption on the same rail.

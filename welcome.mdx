TetraFi delivers **firm RFQ liquidity** - solvers and LPs competing under an escrow, delivery-or-refund model - alongside a **multi-source router** that ranks executable routes across DEX, bridge, issuer, and fiat liquidity. Built on immutable contracts your institution deploys and owns, connected to a shared settlement network - professional-grade execution, under your own regulatory perimeter.

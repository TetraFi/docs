In a custody account, someone else holds the funds and moves them on your instruction. TetraFi sends the instructions and reads the balances; the venue's or custodian's own approvals still apply. You connect each one with the key it issued you.

| Kind | Shown as | Examples |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Venue account**: your exchange or OTC account | "Held at" the venue | "Held at Binance" |
| **Qualified custodian** | "Held by" the custodian | "Held by BitGo Trust" |

## Connect a venue account

You onboard with the venue off-platform; TetraFi adds no step or approval. In **Counterparties**, open the liquidity provider and add the API key the venue issued you on its row. TetraFi checks the key and reads the addresses whitelisted at the venue into Treasury, and trading turns on.

One venue account belongs to one workspace. Step by step, with funding: [Connect a venue account](/before-you-trade#connect-a-venue-account).

## Connect a qualified custodian

| Custodian | Status |
| --- | --- |
| BitGo Trust | Available: connect it in **Settings › Integrations** with the access token and wallet ID BitGo issued you |

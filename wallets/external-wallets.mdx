An external wallet keeps its key on your own device: a browser extension, a mobile wallet or a hardware wallet. It signs only when you connect it, and you sign every transfer in the wallet yourself. TetraFi never signs for it.

There are two ways to add one, both from **Treasury › Accounts › Add a wallet**, then the external wallet:

| Way | Result | It can |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Connect and sign](#connect-and-sign) | **Linked** | Receive, be watched, send when you sign, and receive withdrawals from venues |
| [Paste an address](#paste-an-address) | **Not linked** | Receive and be watched only |

## Connect and sign

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect">
    Connect the wallet that holds the key. EVM, Solana and Tron wallets can connect.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Type and networks">
    Check the wallet's type and tick the networks to switch on. Tick at least one. You can add more later.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign">
    Review the message in your wallet and sign it. You sign one message for the address; it covers every network you ticked, and your wallet stays on whatever network it is on. Signing moves no money. If you are asked, confirm it is you first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name">
    Give it a name your team sees in Accounts, for example *Ops reserve*. The wallet reads **Linked**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="If something goes wrong">
    - **You decline the signature**: nothing is linked and no money moves. Try again, or use a different wallet.
    - **The answer is lost**: TetraFi checks the same request again. Don't sign a new message.
    - **The wallet is linked to another workspace**: it is refused, without naming that workspace. One wallet belongs to one workspace.
    - **The wallet was already linked here**: nothing new is signed; the existing wallet is shown.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Paste an address

Choose the wallet's type (EVM, Solana, Tron or Bitcoin), paste the address, tick its networks and name it. Nothing is signed. The wallet reads **Not linked**: you can receive to it and see its balance, but it can't send, approve, or receive withdrawals from venues.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Link it later">
    Open the wallet's page and link it by signing with the wallet that holds the key. Its type, networks and name are already recorded, so only the signature remains.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Pasting the same address again">
    The same address answers the wallet already recorded, never a second one. Use it to add networks.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Bitcoin">
    A Bitcoin address must be a mainnet address (starting with `1`, `3` or `bc1`). A Bitcoin wallet stays watch only: TetraFi reads its balance and never sends from it.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<Note>Never share your key or recovery phrase. Linking needs only a signature.</Note>

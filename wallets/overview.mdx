TetraFi works with four kinds of account, split by **who can move the funds**. TetraFi never moves funds without the owners' approval, or a limit they approved.

## Four categories

| Category | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| [**External wallet**](/wallets/external-wallets) | You hold the key. It signs only when you connect it. |
| **Hosted wallet** ([Turnkey](/wallets/hosted-turnkey), [Privy](/wallets/hosted-privy)) | A provider runs the key. It can't move funds without your owners' approval or a limit they set. |
| [**Custody account**](/wallets/custody-accounts) | Someone else holds the funds and moves them on your instruction: a qualified custodian, or your account at a venue. |
| **Bank account** | Your company's own bank account. TetraFi shows no bank balance and moves no money from it. |

## Whose provider account

A hosted wallet runs in an account at its provider: **Provided by TetraFi** (no contract of your own; TetraFi holds no owner role) or **Bring your own account** (connected in **Treasury › Providers**). Each wallet shows which, such as "Runs on Privy · Provided by TetraFi".

## Who controls the funds

A hosted wallet's **owners** form its **owner group**; the **threshold** is how many must approve a payment, such as 2 of 3. Each approves with their own sign-in methods or one of their wallets, checked by the provider. The provider runs the key and enforces the owners' rules, but never moves funds alone. TetraFi prepares requests, or signs within limits the owners approved ([its role](/wallets/manage#tetrafis-role-on-a-wallet)). One owner ("just you") is allowed, but if they lose their sign-in methods, nothing can be approved.

## Linked or Not linked

**Linked**: ownership is proven, by a signature or by the provider. **Not linked**: a pasted address; it can receive and be watched, but never sends, approves or counts as the holder until linked from its wallet page.

## Networks

A wallet has one type (EVM, Solana or Tron), one address, and [networks](/wallets/networks) you switch on and off. Bitcoin addresses are watched only.

## What each provider supports

Turnkey and Privy wallets are created and approved inside TetraFi.

<Accordion title="Capabilities by provider">

## The rule that holds everywhere

No API key, admin session, provider connection or gas sponsor creates wallet authority; ownership is checked before every send.

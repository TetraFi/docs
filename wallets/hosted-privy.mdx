A Privy wallet is a [hosted wallet](/wallets/overview): Privy runs the key in a secure enclave, and your owners approve every payment. It shows as "Runs on Privy". Privy wallets are EVM or Solana.

| Connection | Set up | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Provided by TetraFi** | Nothing. Wallets live in the Privy account TetraFi operates, with no contract or bill from Privy. | Available |
| **Bring your own account** | Connect your Privy app. The provider relationship, dashboard and billing are yours. | Available |

In both, the wallet's owners are your people. TetraFi's account holds no owner role.

## Use Privy provided by TetraFi

There is nothing to connect. When you create a wallet, choose **Privy · Provided by TetraFi**. Its owners approve with their own sign-in methods, checked by Privy.

## Connect your own Privy app

You need **Manage credentials**, the app's ID and an app secret from the Privy dashboard (**App settings**).

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start the connection">
    In **Treasury › Providers**, under Privy, choose **Connect your Privy app**. Enter the **Privy app ID**, the **App secret** and, if you like, a name for it in TetraFi. The secret is write-only: TetraFi never shows it again.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose what TetraFi may do">
    **Read them**: use a read-only app secret. TetraFi sees every wallet in the app and can move nothing.

    **Read them and prepare payments for your owners**: use a full-access app secret. TetraFi uses it only on wallets whose owners approve each request.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect">
    Choose **Connect** and confirm with your second factor. TetraFi checks the app ID and secret with Privy and, if Privy refuses them, says what it refused.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why a full-access secret needs care">
    Privy has no narrower secret. A full-access secret can move funds from every wallet in the app that has **no owner**. Wallets created through TetraFi always have owners, so it cannot move theirs alone. Keep wallets without owners out of an app you connect this way.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Replace the secret">
    In **Treasury › Providers**, choose **Rotate key** on the connection and enter a new app secret of the same Privy app. Then retire the old secret in the Privy dashboard.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

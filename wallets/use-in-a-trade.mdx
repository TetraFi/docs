Every trade pays from one wallet or account and delivers to one. Each section below shows both paths: in the app, and with an API key.

## Pick pay-from and deliver-to

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="In the app">
    Each quote on **Trade** shows **Pay from** and **Receive in**. Open either to pick from your wallets and accounts: organisation wallets you may trade from, your connected browser wallet, and your custody accounts. Your choices start from **Settings › Trading defaults**.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="With the API">
    `sourceOptions` on each quote lists the `financialAccountId` values the key may name. Name the paying wallet per network in `source.tradingWallets`, and the output in `deliverTo`. Details: [Choose wallets and accounts](/trade/wallets-and-accounts).

## Who signs

| Pays from | In the app | With an API key |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Venue account** | No wallet signature | No wallet signature; the key needs [venue trading](/authentication#trade-on-your-venue-accounts) |
| **Hosted wallet** | TetraFi signs within the trading grant the owners approved for the venue | The same, once the wallet is [granted to the key](/before-you-trade#give-a-key-organisation-wallets) |
| **External wallet** | You sign each trade in your wallet | [Rolling out](/trade/sign-with-your-own-wallet) |
| **Custody account at a custodian** | A custody request; the custodian's approval applies | Not available |

## Before the order

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="In the app">
    The wallet needs its owners' trading grant for the venue, and the trade's network switched on for it ([Networks](/wallets/networks)). If the network is off, the refusal offers to switch it on.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="With the API">
    `GET /api/v1/me` shows what the key can use now:

    - `grants[].financialAccountId`: the wallet is granted to the key.
    - `grants[].grantedNetworks`: the networks the grant covers. A switch on never extends a grant.
    - `grants[].networks`: which of the wallet's networks are switched on. The key acts only where both agree.
    - `blockedBy`: when present, the code an organisation-wallet order would be refused with now.

    An Advanced quote adds a `preflight` step that returns `walletAuthorization`; Advanced is not open yet.

</Tabs>

## Order, steps and settlement

Placing the order, answering its steps on `POST /api/v1/orders/{orderId}/steps/{stepId}` and following it are on [Orders](/trade/orders) and [How a trade runs](/trade/how-a-trade-runs). Settlement is on [Settlement and credit](/trade/settlement-and-credit).

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="In the app">
    An on-chain trade delivers to the wallet in **Receive in**. A venue trade's output stays on the venue account unless you pick a wallet that is an approved withdrawal destination there; TetraFi then withdraws it once the order fills.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="With the API">
    An on-chain trade delivers to `deliverTo`, or your default. A venue trade's output always stays on the venue account: only a signed-in member delivers it to a wallet ([Where the output goes](/trade/wallets-and-accounts#where-the-output-goes)).

## Refusals

| Code | Meaning and what to do |
| --- | --- |
| `WALLET_NETWORK_OFF` | The network is switched off for the wallet. Switch it on in the app; the grant works again unchanged. |
| `API_KEY_ORGANISATION_WALLET_NETWORK_NOT_GRANTED` | The key's grant doesn't name this network. Have the grant extended to it. |
| `WALLET_NETWORK_NOT_IN_FAMILY` | The network is of another type than the wallet. Choose one of its networks. |
| `WALLET_NETWORK_NOT_REPORTED` | The custodian hasn't reported this network. Choose another network or wallet. |
| `WALLET_NETWORKS_UNAVAILABLE` | The wallet's networks couldn't be read. Try again shortly. |
| `apiKeyOrganisationWalletScopeRequired` | The key holds no wallet grant. Grant it an organisation wallet. |
| `onchainWalletNotUsable` | The saved wallet can't be used by this key. Grant the key a wallet, or choose another. |
| `signingGrantRequired` | The wallet has no trading grant for this venue. Its owners approve one in the app. |

Every code: [Error codes](/reference/error-codes).

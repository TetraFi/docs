title: "Agents"

---

An agent is a service account with a mandatory human **sponsor**, an expiry, and a **kill switch** that freezes every permission it holds. If the sponsor leaves the workspace, the agent is suspended. Agents are a use of the rails, not a product apart from them - and many small agent payments are exactly where [Multilateral Netting](/netting) pays most, so agent netting is designed in from the start (coming soon).

## The Envelope

One agent, one wallet. The envelope carries per-asset caps, a velocity rule, a destination allowlist, a chain allowlist, and an active window. The mandate compiles into clauses placed where they can actually be enforced - by the wallet provider where it can, at TetraFi's gateway otherwise - and the console says which, per clause. A reconciler detects drift between the two. Every agent transfer is checked at the gateway before any signer is asked.

## What Agents Do Today

Transfers, rebalancing to target balances, and per-call service payments under a per-call cap and a rolling window. One audit feed unions the agent's events with the transfers it caused. Rolling out.

## Next

Scheduled payouts and invoice payment appear in the console with their mandate shape. Agent netting: receipts in, net positions out, only the residual settles over the rails (coming soon).

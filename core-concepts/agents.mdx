---
title: "Agents"
description: "A sponsored service account inside a spending envelope, with a kill switch."
---

Agents are rolling out. An agent is a service account with a mandatory human **sponsor**, an expiry, and a **kill switch** that freezes every permission it holds; if the sponsor leaves the workspace, the agent is suspended.

| An agent gets | Bound by |
| --- | --- |
| One wallet | Its envelope: caps, velocity, allowlists, an active window |
| Transfers | They still clear the [treasury policy](/core-concepts/treasury) |
| No wallet authority | Its key is never wallet authority ([Wallets & Signing](/core-concepts/wallets-and-signing)) |

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The envelope">
    One agent, one wallet. The envelope carries per-asset caps, a velocity rule, destination and chain allowlists, and an active window. Each clause is enforced by the wallet provider where possible and at TetraFi's gateway otherwise, and the console says which. Every agent transfer is checked at the gateway before any signer is asked.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What agents do">
    Transfers, rebalancing to target balances, and per-call service payments under a per-call cap and a rolling window, with one audit feed of the agent's events and the transfers it caused (rolling out). Next: scheduled payouts, invoice payment, and [agentic netting](/netting-api/agentic-netting), where only the net residual settles (coming soon).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Agents and netting">
    Agents are a use of the rails, not a product apart from them. Many small agent payments are where [Multilateral Netting](/netting) pays most, so [agentic netting](/netting/agentic-netting) is designed in from the start (coming soon).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

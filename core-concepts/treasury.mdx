The workspace's money desk: custody connections, vault accounts, wallets, transfers, and the written policy every transfer must clear. All of it is readable, and the transfer is drivable, with an API key.

## What It Holds

Custody provider connections are registered in the dashboard only, because they carry secret material. Under them sit vault accounts with per-asset balances and history, registered and embedded wallets, and an address book of saved counterparties.

## Moving Funds

One transfer resource. The source is a vault account or a registered wallet. The destination is a one-time address, a linked wallet, a workspace wallet, a custodian-whitelisted wallet, or an OTC desk's deposit address. Send an idempotency key.

The policy is written, versioned, and can run in **shadow** before it enforces: a whitelist board, an approval quorum with an approvals inbox, a velocity budget per principal, an approval window, and every failure as data - retry, or replace a stuck transfer. The custodian's own approval is separate from the workspace's, and both are shown. (Policy, statements: rolling out.)

## Programmatic Access

Two scopes - read everything, write the transfer - and two gates. A request must carry the scope, and the acting identity's live workspace permission must allow it; both are re-derived on every request, and a refusal names which gate refused.

## Desks and Corridors

Exchange and OTC desks are settlement lanes: deposit intents, T+1 balances, credit utilisation, and an exception queue grouped by cause. Fiat corridors run through bank accounts, personal or company, per ramp provider (rolling out).

## Statements

A custodian ledger reconciles each statement window against your records, within a fee tolerance you set.

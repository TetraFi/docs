title: "Compliance Policy"
description: "One policy, composed per deployment and controlled by your institution - enforced on-chain at the escrow, and the same gate on every layer."
---

Every TetraFi deployment runs under one **compliance policy**: composed per deployment, controlled by your institution, and enforced **on-chain** at the escrow against attested credentials. It is not a review step beside the trade. The check runs inside the settlement path, so every compliance decision is cryptographically verifiable and the audit trail *is* the settlement record.

## Where It Is Enforced

| Point | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| **The request** | Your RFQ requests reach workspace-approved LPs only, with jurisdiction filtering applied before a request ever reaches a desk. If no eligible LP can quote, you get a typed product-unavailable answer, never a silent fallback to another source. |
| **The credential** | Attested credentials travel with the caller (the `X-TetraFi-Attestation` header, see [Authentication](/core-concepts/authentication)), so the policy has something verifiable to check, not a self-declared flag. |
| **The deposit** | `ComplianceSettlerEscrow` enforces the policy at deposit time, when your router locks the input under the signed order. Its output-side twin gates fills on the destination chain. See [Settlement & Smart Contracts](/core-concepts/settlement-smart-contracts). |

A trade that fails the policy is refused before any value moves. There is no post-trade unwind, because there is no state in which non-compliant funds sit in escrow.

## What You Compose

The policy is yours to shape, and it attaches to your deployment rather than to the app calling it:

* **Your compliance pipeline** - checks added, removed, or layered to fit your regulatory perimeter, automated on-chain and off-chain under one policy
* **Your counterparty perimeter** - which LPs and jurisdictions your requests reach, and which venues your router may touch
* **Onboard once** - one verified onboarding pack, reused for KYB with every counterparty on the network
* **One audit trail** - enforcement evidence shared across parties instead of every party repeating the same manual reviews

Because policy attaches to the capability, an asset that may only be held by qualifying investors, or only in certain jurisdictions, stays gated wherever it is routed.

## In the Workspace

The pre-trade gate runs on every order, in a fixed order: sanctions screening of originator and beneficiary, deposit screening, jurisdiction and corridor rules, travel-rule data checks, then your workspace overlay. The **Compliance Center** is where you compose that overlay, tighten-only: workflows that run in **shadow** before they enforce, sanctions evidence with recorded overrides, entity profiles per persona, and reporting from one place. Every trade leaves an evidence pack in a tamper-evident ledger. (Gate and center live; workflow builder and reporting rolling out.)

## The Same Gate on the Next Layers

<Note>
  **Coming soon.** [Multilateral Netting](/netting) and the [Shared Collateral Network](/collateral) are in design. The enforcement points below describe the planned model.
</Note>

The policy you compose for the rails is the one the next two primitives run under, not a second one:

* **Netting** - membership is compliance-gated (KYB'd participants, jurisdiction-aware, per policy domain), checks run at membership and at every run entry, and compliance can only ever exclude a pairing, never redirect money somewhere new
* **Shared collateral** - every vault draw re-runs the policy: KYB'd counterparties, jurisdiction filters, and machine-checkable policy on every draw, the same enforcement that gates trading, not a bolt-on

Every deployment runs under one **compliance policy**, composed and controlled by your institution. A trade that fails it is refused before any value moves, so there is no post-trade unwind.

| Point | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| **The request** | Requests reach only LPs your workspace approved, filtered by jurisdiction |
| **The credential** | An attested credential: something verifiable, not a self-declared flag |
| **The deposit** (Coming soon, with Advanced execution) | The escrow enforces the policy when your router locks the input |

<Accordion title="Each point in detail">
  - **The request.** RFQ requests reach workspace-approved LPs only, jurisdiction-filtered before they reach a desk. If no eligible LP can quote, you get a typed product-unavailable answer, never a silent fallback.
  - **The deposit.** With [Advanced execution](/core-concepts/settlement-smart-contracts), which is not open yet, `ComplianceSettlerEscrow` is designed to enforce the policy **on-chain** when your router locks the input under the signed order; its output-side twin gates fills on the destination chain. The check runs inside the settlement path, so the audit trail *is* the settlement record, and non-compliant funds never sit in escrow.
</Accordion>

## In the Workspace

Every order clears a fixed pre-trade gate, then your own overlay, which you compose in the **Compliance Center**. Every trade leaves an evidence pack in a tamper-evident ledger.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The gate, in order">
    Sanctions screening of originator and beneficiary, deposit screening, jurisdiction and corridor rules, travel-rule checks, then your workspace overlay.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The Compliance Center">
    It composes that overlay, tighten-only: workflows that run in **shadow** before they enforce, sanctions evidence with recorded overrides, entity profiles, reporting. Workflow builder and reporting are rolling out.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What you compose">
    The policy attaches to your deployment, not to the app calling it:

    - **Your compliance pipeline** - checks added, removed, or layered to fit your regulatory perimeter
    - **Your counterparty perimeter** - which LPs, jurisdictions, and venues your requests and router may reach
    - **Onboard once** (coming soon) - one verified onboarding pack, reused for KYB with every counterparty
    - **One audit trail** - enforcement evidence shared across parties instead of repeated manual reviews

    An asset restricted to qualifying investors or jurisdictions stays gated wherever it is routed.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Netting and shared collateral (coming soon)">
    [Multilateral Netting](/netting) and the [Shared Collateral Network](/collateral) are in design. The policy you compose for the rails is the one both will run under:

    - **Netting** - membership is compliance-gated (KYB'd participants, jurisdiction-aware, per policy domain), checks run at every run entry, and compliance can only exclude a pairing, never redirect money
    - **Shared collateral** - every vault draw re-runs the policy: KYB'd counterparties, jurisdiction filters, machine-checkable policy
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

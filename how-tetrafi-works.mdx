## Two Products, One Platform

TetraFi exposes two distinct trading products over one shared routing, settlement, and compliance engine, each optimized for different execution requirements:

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="RFQ - firm liquidity">
    Every eligible solver and LP in your workspace competes to fill your request with a cryptographically firm, executable quote. Settlement runs through an escrow with a delivery-or-refund guarantee - what you see is what you get, and a quote that can't deliver refunds instead of failing you.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Router - aggregated execution">
    The router fans your request across every eligible execution source - direct DEX liquidity, the RFQ venue, bridges, issuers, and fiat rails - and returns ranked executable candidates with an explicit recommendation. Each candidate states its own firmness, atomicity, settlement guarantee, and fees.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

Both products share one platform and one flow: you request quotes, competing sources answer, you sign the winning quote, submit the order, and settlement happens on-chain without TetraFi taking custody.

The key difference is **the contract you buy**:

* **RFQ:** Only firm solver/LP escrow quotes - a closed source set with a delivery-or-refund guarantee
* **Router:** Best execution across heterogeneous sources - guarantees stated per candidate, never assumed

A Router trade may well be *filled* by RFQ liquidity - but the product you called is still Router. Product and execution source are independent: the product defines your guarantees, the source records how it executed.

## Infrastructure You Own

TetraFi builds the settlement rails and modular on-chain primitives for institutions moving on-chain - neobanks, exchanges, fintechs, and asset managers. The core thesis: **immutable contracts that each institution deploys, owns, and governs itself**. Your workspace gets its own on-chain router - no custody key, no upgrade key, capability upgrades only ever add to an allowlist - while connecting you to a shared, globally accessible liquidity and settlement network that grows deeper, safer, and more efficient as participation grows.

TetraFi is not built to replace banks, fintechs, asset managers, or exchanges. It is the shared institutional infrastructure they build on - much as Morpho did for on-chain lending - so that through one integration, an institution can assemble and distribute financial products under its own brand and regulatory perimeter: cross-currency stablecoin accounts, 24/7 payments and remittances, on-chain FX, and, ahead, savings, credit, and tokenised investments.

## Picking Your Product

|                     | **RFQ API - Firm Escrow Liquidity**                                  | **Router API - Aggregated Multi-Source**                                                   |
| ------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Execution model** | Firm quotes only; escrow delivery-or-refund guarantee                | Ranked executable candidates; firmness and guarantees stated per candidate                 |
| **Settlement**      | On-chain DvP via audited escrow contracts; TetraFi never holds custody | On-chain via audited smart contracts; TetraFi never holds custody                            |
| **Optimal for**     | Corridors where firm pricing and delivery guarantees matter most     | Broad coverage, cross-chain routes, or when maximizing source diversity is priority        |
| **Trade-off**       | Limited to assets where solvers/LPs actively quote                   | Guarantee strength varies by candidate; read each candidate's stated terms                 |

**Use RFQ API when:**

* You need firm quotes backed by a delivery-or-refund escrow
* You are trading supported corridors at size
* Predictable settlement guarantees are critical

**Use Router API when:**

* You need broad coverage across DEX, bridge, issuer, and fiat sources
* You want ranked candidates with an explicit recommendation
* You prefer choosing per trade between speed, price, and trust profile

**Use both when:**

* You want firm escrow execution for core corridors and routed coverage for everything else
* One source being down should never keep a valid trade from executing

A search asks every venue your workspace can use. These fields narrow or reorder it:

| Field | Value | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `sell.chain`, `buy.chain` | A CAIP-2 chain id | That side trades on that chain. |
| `filters.locations` | Any of `singleChain`, `crossChain`, `offChain` | Keeps only quotes that execute there. |
| `filters.modalities` | Any of `amm`, `solver`, `bridge`, `rfq`, `orderBook`, `fiat` | Keeps only venues of those types. |
| `filters.venues` | `{"include": [...], "exclude": [...]}` | Asks only the venues you name, or every venue except them. An empty `include` keeps every venue. |
| `filters.variants` | `{"sell": "nativeOnly", "buy": "nativeOnly"}`, each `any` by default | Keeps only the native form of that side's asset. |
| `filters.firmOnly` | `true` or `false` (default) | Keeps only firm quotes ([Firmness and lifetime](/search/firmness-and-lifetime#ask-for-firm-prices)). |
| `sort` | `netReceived` (default) or `eta` | The order of `quotes`. |
| `priceProtectionBps` | 0 to 1000, 30 when omitted | How far a non-firm executable quote may deliver below its quote. |

A filter you leave out keeps everything. A venue must pass every filter you send to be asked. A venue that does not is listed in `sources` with `status: "filteredOut"` and `asked: false`, so you can always see what you left out; when nothing is left, `quotes` is empty and `sources` shows why.

The cURL blocks below are excerpts from two quote-only scripts, [`search-basics.sh`](https://github.com/tetrafi/docs/blob/main/samples/http/search-basics.sh) (chains) and [`search-filters.sh`](https://github.com/tetrafi/docs/blob/main/samples/http/search-filters.sh) (everything else). Each defines `sell`, `buy`, `amount` and its signed request helper; run them with the [Quickstart](/quickstart#set-your-environment) environment.

## Chains and routes

An asset such as `USDC` carries no chain. Left open, a side can trade on any chain the asset is deployed on, or on your venue accounts. Take chain ids from the deployments in [`GET /api/v1/assets`](/search/how-it-works#use-values-from-discovery).

| `location.kind` | Meaning | Its sides |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `singleChain` | Swapped on one chain. | `sell.chain` and `buy.chain` are the same. |
| `crossChain` | Moved between chains by a bridge or a solver. | `sell.chain` and `buy.chain` differ. |
| `offChain` | Traded on one of your venue accounts. | `chain` is `null` on a side held on the account. |
| `mixed` | Legs that execute in different places. Each leg has its own location. | Not a filter value: sending it is `400 INVALID_REQUEST`. |

Single-chain: pin both sides to the same chain and keep `singleChain`.

Cross-chain: pin the two sides to different chains. To move one asset between chains, put the same asset on both sides and pin at least one. A cross-chain quote's `eta` is the expected delivery time, such as `PT10S`.

Off-chain: keep `offChain` and pin no chain. Only your venue accounts answer.

## Venue types and venues

Every venue has one type, in `venue.modality` on each source and quote leg: `amm` (an on-chain pool), `solver` (fills your order on-chain), `bridge` (moves an asset between chains), `rfq` (a desk that quotes on request through your venue account), `orderBook` (an exchange book, through your venue account) or `fiat` (between bank money and digital assets). New types can appear: treat one you do not recognise as "other".

<SearchModalities />

`filters.venues` matches `venue.id`, the id of the route that executes, such as `binance-orderbook`. It does not match `venue.venueId`, the named venue, such as `binance`, which can have several routes. Take ids from `sources[].venue.id` or a [price](/search/prices) row's `venue.id`.

```json
{ "status": "answered", "venue": { "id": "binance-orderbook", "venueId": "binance", "modality": "orderBook" }, "asked": true }
```

<SearchVenueInclude />

<SearchVenueExclude />

Naming a desk in `filters.venues.include` is also how you ask it for a firm price when it quotes only on request; its source says so in `detail` when a broad search did not ask it.

## Native only

An asset can exist in several variants, listed by `GET /api/v1/assets` with a `kind`: `native` (the asset itself), `bridged` (such as `USDC.e`), `wrapped` (such as `WETH`), `omnichain` or `test`. Treat a kind you do not recognise as not native. Each quote states its variants in `sell.variant` and `buy.variant`.

`filters.variants` with `nativeOnly` acts on each venue's routes, not on whole venues: a venue whose only route uses another variant is not asked and reads `unavailable` with the reason `onchainAssetVariantFiltered`. For example, a pool that pays out WETH drops out of a search for ETH with `buy: "nativeOnly"`, while venue accounts holding native ETH still quote.

## Firm only

`filters.firmOnly: true` keeps only quotes with `quality: "firm"`. It filters after venues answer, so the same venues are asked.

## Sort

| `sort` | Amount you send | Order of `quotes` |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `netReceived` | `sellAmount` | Most `netReceived` first. |
| `netReceived` | `buyAmount` | Least `sell.amount` first. |
| `eta` | Either | Shortest `eta` first. |

A quote whose value cannot be read goes last: an `unavailable` `netReceived`, or no `eta` (venue account quotes may carry none). Ties go first to quotes whose every fee is known, then by venue id.

<SearchSortEta />

## Price protection

A non-firm executable quote may deliver a little less than it quotes. `priceProtectionBps` sets the most it may fall, in basis points (100 is 1%), and the quote states the result:

| Quote | Field | Value |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `executable` | `minOut` | The least you receive: the quoted amount less the protection, rounded down to the buy amount's precision. |
| Buy exact, from a venue account | `maxPay` | The most you sell: once the venue has firmly priced it, its amount raised by the protection and rounded up; until then, `sell.amount`. |
| `firm` or `indicative` | `minOut` | `null`. |

<SearchPriceProtection />

For example, 100 basis points on a quote to buy `0.4322` ETH gives a `minOut` of `0.4278`:

```json Output
{ "venue": "...", "buy": "0.4322", "minOut": "0.4278" }
```

A [requote](/trade/requotes-and-expiry) can send its own `priceProtectionBps`, or keep the original's. An order on a Basic quote takes no `priceProtectionBps` (`400 INVALID_REQUEST`): set your floor with `minReceive` instead ([Orders](/trade/orders#minimum-receive)).

## Refusals

| Code | Why | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `SAME_ASSET_NEEDS_CHAIN` | The same asset on both sides with nothing to tell them apart: no chain pinned, or the same chain with `nativeOnly` on both sides. | Pin a chain on at least one side, or two different chains. |
| `CHAIN_PIN_NEEDS_ON_CHAIN` | A side is pinned to a chain, but `filters.locations` allows only `offChain`. | Remove the pin, or allow `singleChain` or `crossChain`. |
| `ASSET_NOT_ON_CHAIN` | The pinned chain has no deployment of that asset; `details.value` is the chain. | Pin a chain the asset is deployed on. |
| `UNKNOWN_CHAIN`, `UNKNOWN_VENUE` | A chain or venue id TetraFi does not know; `details.value` names it. | Use values from `GET /api/v1/assets` and `sources[].venue.id`. |
| `INVALID_REQUEST` | A filter value is not one listed above, `filters.locations` includes `mixed`, `firmOnly` is not a boolean, `sort` is unknown, or `priceProtectionBps` is outside 0 to 1000. `message` names the field. | Fix that field. |

title: "Quote lifetime and firmness"
description: "Read the deadline on each quote, and distinguish a binding price from an estimate."
---

Use the quote's `expiresAt`, not a timer started when your client receives it. It is the last time an executable quote can be ordered, not its settlement deadline. An indicative quote has `expiresAt: null`; its `observedAt` and `staleAfter` describe price freshness instead.

## Windows by source

There is no single lifetime for a source family. These are the current boundaries; network time, an older observation or an earlier provider deadline can leave less time in the response.

| Source | How its window is set |
| --- | --- |
| Exchange or OTC RFQ | The venue's own quote terms and expiry apply. For example, Keyrock declares a 3-second lifetime and Binance RFQ declares 30 seconds; these are not a promise that your client receives that much time. |
| Order book | At most 5 seconds from pricing, also bounded by the book's remaining freshness and the request deadline. It is an executable estimate, not a reserved book price. |
| AMM or DEX | Route-specific. A Basic Uniswap quote is held for at most 60 seconds. Protocol quotes use the route's returned deadline; do not apply the Basic window to every DEX. |
| Solver | The solver's returned validity sets the deadline. There is no family-wide fixed window. |
| Bridge | Native CCTP quotes have a 60-second window. Basic Across and Relay quotes are capped at 60 seconds, or their provider expiry if earlier. Other routes use their returned deadline. |
| Indicative | No orderable expiry: `expiresAt` is `null`. Refresh after `staleAfter`, or request an executable quote before ordering. |

Always read the returned deadline, including after a requote. [Requotes and expiry](/trade/requotes-and-expiry) explains how to refresh a quote without searching every venue again.

## What firmness promises

- `firm: true` means the venue is bound to the quoted price until `expiresAt`.
- `lastLook: false` means the venue does not re-check an accepted price before filling: an order inside the firm quote's window trades at that price or is refused unsent.
- `firm: false` is not a price commitment. An order-book estimate or AMM route can reprice within its stated protection.

Firmness does not waive funding, wallet, permission or pre-trade checks. It does not promise that an order will be accepted, that settlement is immediate, or that the quote is bonded. Follow the quote's `next` action and then the [order's status](/trade/order-status); see [Firm quotes only](/search/firm-only) to filter out non-firm prices.

## The Prefunding Tax

A PSP or trading desk today parks balances at every venue it might need - each CEX sub-account, each OTC desk, each corridor. Capital scales with venue count, not with flow, and most of it sits idle waiting for the day it is needed.

## One Credit Layer, Every Venue

Venue Prefunding replaces the parked balances with one committed capital layer: the desk executes at the venue now and repays as its own collections arrive. Draws are secured by the settlement objects they fund plus haircuts, inside a hard envelope - corridor limits, intraday caps, automatic stop-outs - with every participant KYB'd and policy-gated like everywhere else on the network.

* **Execute at flow size, fund at net size** - capital follows actual settlement demand instead of being multiplied across venues
* **One counterparty relationship** - a single credit layer under all venues, instead of a prefunding negotiation per venue
* **Custody-aware by design** - venue balances are the one case where money leaves the chain, so the product is built around custodial and legal enforcement, not just code

This is the network's lane for [payment networks](/use-cases/payment-networks) and desks running corridor flow at scale.

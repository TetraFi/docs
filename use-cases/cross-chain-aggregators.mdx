The hard part of a cross-chain route isn't the bridge - it's knowing what the far side will pay before you commit the near side. TetraFi locks both legs with firm commitments backed by real inventory - deepest on [stablecoin and tokenized-asset corridors](/supported-chains) - so the destination price is known at departure, and the same integration behaves identically on every network.

## What You Get

* **Escrow-backed depth on both sides** of the route, never best-effort
* **The far side is known at departure** - no post-bridge repricing risk
* **Corridor-agnostic integration** - one codepath, every supported network

## The venue your router is missing

Your router already knows every pool. What it's missing is the liquidity that never touches one: institutional inventory, priced by desks that actually hold it, for [regulated stablecoins and tokenized real-world assets](/supported-chains) at institutional size, between [counterparties you can KYB](/why-tetrafi).

<Accordion title="What you get">
  Broad aggregators deliver reach across the long tail; TetraFi is purpose-built for the narrower segment your highest-value flow lives in. Add the RFQ venue for that segment; your routing keeps the rest.

  - **Identified counterparties, not a pool.** Every fill comes from a KYB'd desk, restricted to the LPs and jurisdictions you allow, so your users inherit the compliance.
  - **Firm at any size.** LP inventory is quoted, not modeled, so the number holds when the ticket grows.
  - **Any chain in, one signature** (coming soon, with Advanced execution). No bridge menus in your UX, and escrow enforces delivery or refund.
  - **Monetization that rides along.** An optional fee inside each quote, nothing extra to build.
  - **Live indicative [prices](/search/prices)** to steer route selection, and ranked routes across every source when coverage matters most.
</Accordion>

## Netting on top

Coming soon: [Multilateral Netting](/netting) is in design; parameters publish at launch.

<Accordion title="How it will work">
  Your product already settles a book (swaps, transfers, rebalances), and every leg pays the full toll even when an opposite flow crosses minutes later. Netting goes on top of the architecture you have: your own book clears by **atomic multilateral set-off** before it settles, nothing moves onto our rails first, and nothing becomes visible. Positions are readable only inside a [sealed engine](/netting/privacy-and-control), so you plug netting onto your own book without leaking your trades, your counterparties or your edge. An institutional app's opposing trades clear with inventory where it sits; a wallet's daily inbound and outbound stablecoin flow nets to one residual.

  Where value still has to wait, the [Shared Collateral Network](/collateral) (coming soon) funds it: holders exit a yield-bearing position [T+0](/collateral/instant-redemption) and redeem stablecoins when the issuer's banking rails are shut, on vault capital rather than your treasury.
</Accordion>

Whether you ship a consumer wallet, an institutional app, or an aggregator, you already have routing. TetraFi is not a replacement for it. It is two **plug-and-play** additions: a venue for the segment where your routing is weakest, and netting on top of the flow you already settle.

</Note>

## Plug In the Venue Your Router Is Missing

Your router already knows every pool. What it's missing is the liquidity that never touches one: institutional inventory quoted firm, in [stablecoins and tokenized real-world assets](/supported-chains), priced by desks that actually hold them. Add TetraFi's RFQ venue as one more source and it shows up precisely where pools give out - on size, in that segment - so for those trades the shown price is the settled price, reliably, on every supported network.

### What You Get

* **Firm at any size** - LP inventory is quoted, not modeled, so the number holds when the ticket grows
* **Zero-surprise settlement** - escrow enforces delivery or refund, with stuck positions structurally impossible
* **Any chain in, one signature** - users move from whatever they hold into whatever you offer, with no bridge menus or multi-step flows in your UX, and the destination price is known at departure
* **Your counterparty policy, enforced** - quote flow restricted to LPs you've approved, filtered to the jurisdictions you allow, with a unified audit trail behind every fill
* **Monetization that rides along** - an optional fee inside each quote, nothing extra to build

## Plug In Netting On Top

<Note>
  **Coming soon.** [Multilateral Netting](/netting) is in design - parameters publish at launch.
</Note>

The bigger addition is not a venue at all. Your product already settles a book - swaps, transfers, rebalances - and every leg pays the full toll even when an opposite flow crosses minutes later. [Multilateral Netting](/netting) goes on top of the architecture you have: your own book clears by **atomic multilateral set-off** before it settles, only the residual pays a toll, and none of your flow has to move onto our rails first. For an institutional app, a desk's opposing trades clear and inventory stays where it sits; for a wallet or payment app, a day's inbound and outbound stablecoin flow nets to one residual.

Where value still has to wait, the [Shared Collateral Network](/collateral) funds it: [T+0 exits](/collateral/instant-redemption) on yield-bearing balances and stablecoin redemption when the issuer's banking rails are shut, [T+N legs](/collateral/instant-settlement) paid today, and [fills funded](/collateral/solver-credit) beyond one desk's float.

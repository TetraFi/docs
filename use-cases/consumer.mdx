For a consumer product, a swap has exactly one job: settle at the number on the screen. Every revert, every slipped price, every failed transaction chips away at trust that took months to earn. TetraFi routes your users onto firm professional liquidity, so the shown price is the settled price - reliably, invisibly.

## What You Get

* **Zero-surprise settlement** - the price and the fill both hold, trade after trade
* **Consistent behavior everywhere** your users are, across all supported networks
* **Speed-or-price dial** - bias each quote toward whichever your UX values more
* **Coverage that includes** stablecoins and RWA tokens, not just majors
* **Monetization that rides along** - an optional fee inside each quote, nothing extra to build

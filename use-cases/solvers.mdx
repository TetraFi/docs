Solvers compete in intent-based auctions by finding the best execution paths for user orders. Our RFQ platform hands you direct access to institutional-grade market maker liquidity that consistently beats public DEX paths at size.

<Note>
  On the horizon: [**propAMM**](/propamm-beta) will let you execute against coordinated, oracle-priced maker quotes with onchain settlement guarantees.
</Note>

## What You Get

* **Reach deeper liquidity** - large orders are automatically split across multiple market makers for the best price
* **Sharpen your pricing** with short expiry quotes built for auction competitiveness
* **Win time-sensitive auctions** with a streamlined quoting flow that returns a price in 100ms
* **Lift fill rates** with partial fill support - blend AMM and market maker liquidity seamlessly
* **Cut overhead** with fees priced straight into quotes, removing calculation complexity
* **Prepare pre-auction** using our real-time indicative prices for efficient path computation
* **Scale up** with batch quoting across multiple intents at once

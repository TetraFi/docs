Institutional platforms bring their own demands: bigger tickets, counterparty preferences, and multi-asset workflows that don't fit a retail swap box. TetraFi answers with firm pricing that holds up at size, routing you can constrain to chosen counterparties, and portfolio-grade operations settled in a single transaction.

## What You Get

* **Pricing that scales with the ticket** - deep LP inventory quoted firm, not estimated
* **Counterparty control** - constrain quotes to preferred LPs or a known counterparty set
* **One-transaction rebalancing** - multi-token orders settle atomically, ideal for basket trades and one-click portfolio moves
* **Settlement certainty** - escrow-enforced delivery or refund, no reverts, no stuck positions

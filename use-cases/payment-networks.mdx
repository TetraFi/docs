A payment network doesn't swap tokens. It moves value between currencies and chains, at settlement size, under rules someone will ask you about later - and the awkward part is that the pricing, the counterparty, and the compliance evidence all have to hold at once. Broad multi-chain aggregators deliver reach across the long tail; TetraFi is purpose-built for the narrower segment your flow actually lives in - [stablecoin corridors, institutional ticket sizes, identified counterparties](/why-tetrafi).

## The Two Costs That Define a Corridor

<Note>
  **Coming soon.** [Venue Prefunding](/collateral/venue-prefunding) and [Multilateral Netting](/netting) are in design - parameters publish at launch.
</Note>

Corridor economics are set by capital and toll, not by the last basis point of price. Two products go at each directly:

* **Stop prefunding every venue.** Today a balance sits at every CEX and OTC venue, sized for its worst day, so capital scales with venue count rather than with flow. [Venue Prefunding](/collateral/venue-prefunding) collapses them into a single committed credit layer: **execute at flow size, fund at net size**.
* **Stop paying the gross toll.** Every corridor leg settles alone and pays the full toll - bridge, rebalance, conversion - even when the opposite flow crosses minutes later. Balanced north-south flow is exactly the shape that [nets well](/netting/where-it-applies): with [Multilateral Netting](/netting), opposing flows offset each run and **only the residual pays a toll**. It is a primitive you point at the book you already settle - it does not ask you to move flow onto our rails first.

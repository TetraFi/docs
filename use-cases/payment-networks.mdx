Corridor economics are set by **capital**, not by the last basis point of price. Two costs define every corridor: the balances parked at **pre-funded venues**, and the gross toll on flow that mostly opposes itself, since most stablecoin corridor flow is **nettable**. Today you get competing quotes at corridor size on your own venue accounts; the capital layers that go at both costs are coming soon.

| Adopt | Status | What changes | What you keep |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Firm liquidity** | Live | Corridor pricing at settlement size, where pools give out | The aggregated routing you already integrate |
| **[Multilateral Netting](/netting)** | Coming soon | Opposing flows offset each run; only the residual pays a toll | Your settlement rails |
| **[Shared Collateral Network](/collateral)** | Coming soon | [Venue balances](/collateral/venue-prefunding) become one credit layer, [T+N legs](/collateral/instant-settlement) are paid T+0, and idle float earns | Your cycles, your credit lines |

## Capital you stop parking

Coming soon: [PSP Prefunding](/collateral/psp-prefunding) and [Venue Prefunding](/collateral/venue-prefunding) are in design, and [Multilateral Netting](/netting) opens to design partners first; parameters publish at launch.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Stop prefunding every venue">
    Prefunding scales with venue count, not with flow, and most of it sits idle. [Venue Prefunding](/collateral/venue-prefunding) collapses one balance per CEX and OTC venue into a single credit layer: **execute at flow size, fund at net size**, repaid from your own float as collections arrive, T+1, T+7, whatever your cycle is. Custody-aware by design, so it fits the off-exchange settlement you already run.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Pay out before fiat lands">
    Your customer has paid; their fiat is still crossing bank rails. [PSP Prefunding](/collateral/psp-prefunding) draws the stablecoins for the payout now and is repaid when that fiat lands: **credit backed by the payment itself**, verified before capital moves, instead of capital parked in advance in every corridor.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Put the float to work">
    Committed to a permissioned [programmable collateral vault](/collateral/vaults), the float you already hold backs prefunding, settlement and redemptions across the network at once (one unit of capital, many uses) and earns the discount spread on every draw plus the yield inside the obligations it funds. Every vault names its own regulated operator; TetraFi stays infrastructure.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Net before you settle">
    Balanced north-south flow is exactly the shape that [nets well](/netting/trading-and-payments): opposing obligations cancel by **atomic multilateral set-off**, and only the residual pays a toll. That is **capital efficiency** in its plainest form: less capital clears more debt, most of all in the volatile events where prefunded balances run thinnest. Point it at the book you already settle: nothing moves onto our rails first, and nothing is exposed. Positions are readable only inside a [sealed engine](/netting/privacy-and-control), so your corridors and counterparties stay private, even from the rest of the network.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

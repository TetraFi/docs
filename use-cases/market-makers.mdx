## Why Quote on TetraFi

* **Plug into a leading RFQ network on EVM**
* **Flow from every direction:** wallets, aggregators, solvers, and institutional apps in one stream
* **Every network, one integration** - quote across all supported chains from a single connection
* **Firm-quote economics** - you set pricing, spreads, and exposure
* **Both execution paths** - support API-submitted and self-broadcast flows as takers prefer
* **Numbers you can steer by:** fill-rate and trading metrics to tune your quoting strategy

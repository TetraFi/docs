An asset without liquidity is a promise nobody can trade on - and it has to be there in size. Traders expect tight spreads, vault curators need liquidations to clear, and steady volume compounds into growth. Pool-based liquidity buys all that expensively: capital idles, and incentives leak out just to keep it provisioned. TetraFi's RFQ rails deliver the same on-chain availability with an order of magnitude less capital, and route your asset into DeFi-wide distribution from the first day.

## Liquidity Models

Pick the operating model that suits your team:

* **Issuer-run liquidity** - act as your own market maker, keeping on-chain custody of inventory, pricing, and the spread.
* **Delegated market making** - engage one or more LPs from TetraFi's network to quote and manage inventory for you.

## Why It Works

* **Capital efficiency** - dramatically cheaper than funding and rebalancing AMM pools; nothing sits idle
* **Day-one distribution** into every aggregator, solver, liquidator, and wallet already on TetraFi
* **Size without penalty** - firm price and fill at large tickets, fit for trading and liquidations alike
* **Levers stay with you** - spreads, inventory availability, and chain coverage are your calls
* **Fees and rebates** embedded directly in the trade flow when you want them

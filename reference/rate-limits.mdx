On a `429`, wait `Retry-After` seconds, then retry once; for an order, keep the same `Idempotency-Key`. Most refusals also carry `details.retryAfter`, the same wait as an ISO 8601 duration such as `PT3S`.

| Limit | What it counts | Over it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Quotes | One live `POST /api/v1/quotes` every 3 seconds per key. A requote counts; a request answered entirely from cached prices does not. | `429 QUOTE_RATE_LIMITED` |
| Requests per key | Every request, up to the key's `rateLimits` in `GET /api/v1/me` (`requestsPerMinute`, `burstSize`). Costlier operations spend more. | `429 RATE_LIMITED` |
| Requests per address | Requests from one network address, before the key is checked. | `429 RATE_LIMITED` |
| Price reads | Price reads per caller, separate from quotes. | `429 PRICES_RATE_LIMIT` |
| Price streams | At most 4 open price streams per caller. | `429 PRICES_STREAM_LIMIT`: close one first. |

`RATE_LIMITED` also answers, before anything is sent, when TetraFi is briefly at capacity. Then no quota was counted.

## Why quotes are limited

A live quote request asks venues, and each venue limits how often it answers. Watch a market with [prices](/search/prices), and request a quote only when you intend to trade.

| A venue asked too often | What you see |
| --- | --- |
| Stops quoting you for a while | Its source reads [`askLimitReached`](/reference/error-codes#askLimitReached), with a `fix` naming when it reopens, or [`firmQuoteLimited`](/reference/error-codes#firmQuoteLimited). |
| Its own quote budget runs out on a requote | `429 VENUE_FIRM_QUOTE_LIMITED` or `BUDGET_EXHAUSTED`. |

This example sends two quote requests at once, waits for the refused one's `details.retryAfter`, then sends it again. It places no order.

## Read the headers

Any answer a limit counted carries two headers in the [IETF RateLimit header fields](https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/draft-ietf-httpapi-ratelimit-headers/) format:

| Header | Meaning | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `RateLimit-Policy` | The limit and its window in seconds. | `1;w=3`: one request every 3 seconds. |
| `RateLimit` | `limit`; `remaining`, the requests you may send now; `reset`, whole seconds until quota next frees up. | `limit=1, remaining=0, reset=3` |

| Case | What the headers describe |
| --- | --- |
| Several limits counted the request | The one closest to running out: the lowest `remaining`, then the longest `reset`. |
| A `429` | The limit that refused it, with `remaining=0` and `reset` equal to `Retry-After`. |
| A refusal with no known quota, such as a venue's cooldown or TetraFi at capacity | No `RateLimit` headers; `Retry-After` only. |
| An answer no limit counted | Neither header. Treat an absent header as "not counted", never as zero. |

`reset` is a wait, not a timestamp or a promise that the whole allowance refills.

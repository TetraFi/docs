Any flow you route across TetraFi can pay you back. Two fee models are available - use the one that matches how your product works.

## Pick a fee model

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Workspace fee">
    Set a rate once, at the workspace level, as part of your integration agreement. From then on it rides on every quote issued under your keys automatically - zero code, disclosed as an `integrator` line in each quote's `fees`.
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Per-quote override">
    For integrators running several fee tiers over one workspace - say, different rates per corridor or per user segment - tiering is handled in workspace configuration. [Get in touch](/support) to set it up.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## From trade to payout

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set your rate">
    Agree the basis-point rate with TetraFi and have it configured on your workspace - a single flat rate is the usual starting point.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Fees ride inside each quote">
    Quoted receive-amounts already account for your fee; what the taker pays in never changes. Each quote itemises every fee line in `fees`: its kind, who pays it, and whether the quoted receive amount already includes it.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Collect">
    Track fee-bearing volume in your workspace's trade history and settle on the cadence in your agreement.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## One model, every venue

Whichever venue or route fills the trade, the workspace fee applies identically and is disclosed the same way - revenue starts with your first settled trade. On [Multilateral Netting](/netting) and the [Shared Collateral Network](/collateral), when they ship: the netting fee follows the saving, never the gross, and your partner fee stays on the instant quote.

## Finding your number

Rates between 5 and 50 bps are typical, and where you land depends on what you're building and for whom. A heavier fee shows up as a wider effective spread - noticeable to users who shop venues - so many teams open conservatively and let volume data argue for adjustments.

Any flow you route across TetraFi can pay you back. Pick the fee model that matches how your product works.

## Pick a fee model

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Workspace fee">
    Set a rate once, at the workspace level, in your integration agreement. It then rides on every quote issued under your keys, with zero code, disclosed as an `integrator` line in each quote's `fees`.
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Per-quote override">
    Several fee tiers over one workspace, such as different rates per corridor or user segment, are handled in workspace configuration. [Get in touch](/support) to set it up.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## From trade to payout

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set your rate">
    Agree the basis-point rate with TetraFi and have it configured on your workspace. A single flat rate is the usual start.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Fees ride inside each quote">
    Quoted receive amounts already account for your fee; what the taker pays in never changes. Each quote itemises every fee line in `fees`: its kind, who pays it, and whether the quoted receive amount includes it.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Collect">
    Track fee-bearing volume in your workspace's trade history and settle on the cadence in your agreement.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## One model, every venue

- The workspace fee applies identically, and is disclosed the same way, whichever venue or route fills the trade.
- Revenue starts with your first settled trade.
- On [Multilateral Netting](/netting) and the [Shared Collateral Network](/collateral), when they ship, the netting fee follows the saving, never the gross, and your partner fee stays on the instant quote.

## Finding your number

Rates between 5 and 50 bps are typical, depending on what you build and for whom. A heavier fee shows up as a wider effective spread, which users who shop venues notice. Many teams open conservatively and adjust on volume data.

Every trade that flows through TetraFi can generate revenue for your product. There are two ways to collect fees  - choose whichever fits your workflow.

## Pick a fee model

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Workspace fee">
    Agree a fee rate with TetraFi and it is configured on your workspace - applied automatically to every quote under your API keys. No code changes required, and every quote discloses it via `platformFeeBps`.
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Per-quote fee">
    A per-request `fee` override - vary the fee by corridor, trade size, or user tier over a single workspace - is part of the Router API target surface. Until it ships, fee tiers are handled through workspace configuration. See the [implementation guide](/core-concepts/monetization) for details.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## From trade to payout

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose your fee model">
    Agree your rate with TetraFi and have it set on your workspace - most integrators start with a single flat rate.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Trades settle with your fee included">
    The fee is priced into the quote - the taker's receive side already reflects it, and the taker's input amount stays the same. Each quote discloses its components via `platformFeeBps` and `lpSpreadBps`.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Get paid">
    Monitor fee-bearing volume via [Trade History](/trade-history-api/introduction) - results are scoped to your workspace - and settle per your workspace agreement.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Coverage across products

Workspace fees apply on both the [RFQ API](/rfq-api/introduction) and the [Router API](/router-api/introduction), so you can monetize from day one. The disclosure mechanism is the same on both - see the [implementation guide](/core-concepts/monetization) for details.

## Setting the right rate

Most integrators charge between 5 and 50 bps depending on their product and audience. Higher fees widen the quoted spread, which can make your pricing less competitive if your users compare across venues. Starting lower and adjusting based on volume data is a common approach.

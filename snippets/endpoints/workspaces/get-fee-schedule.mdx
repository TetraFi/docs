| **Who** | Any current member. Keys and OAuth clients need `fee_schedule_read`; no other permission includes it. |
| **Rates** | Decimal strings in parts per million: 100 ppm is one basis point. |
| **All or nothing** | Never a partial or zero schedule: an unreadable one answers 503. |

<Accordion title="Fields to read">
| Field | What it tells you |
| --- | --- |
| `current` | The fees in force now: `defaultRates`, and `venues` whose rates differ. |
| `upcoming` | Each scheduled change, with its `effectiveFrom` and the whole schedule from then. |
| `platform`, `grossPlatform` | Your charged rate, and the rate before any taker incentive. |
| `takerIncentive`, `makerShare` | Your rebate, and your share as a partner maker; null if none. |

[Every rate](/trade/balances#fee-schedule).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Refusals">
| Status and code | Why, and what to do |
| --- | --- |
| 400 `INVALID_REQUEST` | The path's workspace id is malformed. |
| 403 `INSUFFICIENT_SCOPE` | The key lacks `fee_schedule_read` (Read fee schedule). |
| 404 `WORKSPACE_NOT_FOUND` | The path's workspace is unknown or not yours. |
| 503 `FEE_SCHEDULE_UNAVAILABLE` | The schedule could not be read. Retry shortly. |
</Accordion>

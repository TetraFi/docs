| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Who** | Company or broker members, and keys with `treasury_read` and View wallets. |
| **Account id** | An item's `accountId` is the `venueAccountId` the account reads take. |
| **Wallet-based** | No `accountId`: `fundingTarget` names its trading wallets instead. |
| **Paging** | Oldest first; `limit` 1 to 100 (default 20); pass `nextCursor` as `cursor` until null. |

<Accordion title="Fields to read">
| Field | What it tells you |
| --- | --- |
| `venueEnablementId` | Key items by this: it is stable across reads, and is not the venue account id. |
| `accountId` | The `venueAccountId` for balances, deposit history and deposit instructions. Absent on a `walletBased` item. |
| `fundingTarget` | Where the money lives: a venue account, or your organisation trading wallets. |
| `lifecycle` | Read balances only from a `configured` account. |

Items also carry the venue, the linked accounts and wallets, execution types, key state, and whether disabling the venue retires its key. [Every field](/trade/balances#list-your-exchange-accounts).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Refusals">
| Status and code | Why, and what to do |
| --- | --- |
| 400 `LIMIT_INVALID` | `limit` is outside 1 to 100; it is never clamped. |
| 400 `CURSOR_INVALID` | The cursor was not issued by this list. Start again without one. |
| 403 `VENUE_READ_AUTHORITY_REFUSED` | Your read authority ended: `authority_ended` (credential revoked, expired or rescoped) or `permission_withdrawn` (View wallets, membership or taker admission lost). An ended session answers 401. |
| 404 `WORKSPACE_NOT_FOUND` | The path names an unknown or foreign workspace. Nothing of it was read. |
</Accordion>

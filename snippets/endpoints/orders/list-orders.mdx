| **Permission** | Key scope `orders_read`; a member who can view or place orders. |
| **Paging** | Up to 20 a page. Follow `nextCursor` until it is `null`. |
| **Short pages** | A filtered page may hold fewer than `limit` items, even none, and still answer `nextCursor`. |

<Accordion title="Filters and order">
Newest first, by created time and id. Each item is the same envelope as `GET /orders/{orderId}` ([Order status](/trade/order-status)).

| Filter | Matches |
| --- | --- |
| `status` | One public status, shared by Basic and Advanced orders. It is checked against each order's current public status. |
| `kind` | `basicTrade` or `advancedTrade`. |
| `from`, `to` | Created at or after, and at or before, an RFC 3339 instant (both inclusive). |
| `sellAsset`, `buyAsset` | The asset sold or bought. |
| `clientOrderId` | Your exact reference, including terminal orders that reused it. |

A filtered page keeps reading until it holds `limit` items or the orders end, within a bounded scan. A scan that stops before the orders end always answers a `nextCursor`, so a short or empty page does not mean you are done.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Refusals">
| Status and code | Why, and what to do |
| --- | --- |
| 400 `INVALID_REQUEST` | An unknown parameter, or an unknown `status` or `kind` value. `details.field` names it. |
| 400 `VALIDATION_ERROR` | `limit` outside 1 to 20, `from` after `to`, or an asset that is not registered. |
| 400 `INVALID_CURSOR` | The cursor is malformed, from another caller or filter set, or its anchor order is gone. Start again without it. |
</Accordion>

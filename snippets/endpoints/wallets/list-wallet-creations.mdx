| **Who sees one** | A personal wallet: its creator. An organisation wallet: its people and members with Manage wallets. |
| **Names you** | Workspace membership is enough to read a wallet that names you. |
| **API key** | `treasury_read`: reads what its member would, as their permissions stand now. |
| **Expiry** | A pending wallet past its expiry reads `expired`. |

<Accordion title="Fields to read">
| Field | What it tells you |
| --- | --- |
| `status` | `pending`, `creating`, `uncertain`, `created`, `active`, `readbackMismatch`, `failed` or `expired`. Filter with it, for example `status=pending`. |
| `missing` | The people who have not proven their factors yet. |
| `createdBy` | Who started the wallet. |
| `signingRequestId` | Your own open signing request on it, when you have one. |
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Waiting for you: assignee=me">
`assignee=me` lists only the wallets waiting for you, at any status:

- your factors to prove, while it is `pending`;
- the created wallet's owners to confirm from your own signing page, while it is `created` or `active`, each with its `signingRequestId`;
- a registered organisation wallet you created whose Trade grant, chosen at creation, is not written yet (`creatorTradeGrant`). `POST …/wallets/{walletId}/reconcile` in your own session writes it.

It never lists one you already confirmed, or one that does not name you.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Refusals">
| Status and code | Why, and what to do |
| --- | --- |
| 404 `WORKSPACE_NOT_FOUND` | The path workspace is unknown, or is not the one you are signed in to. |
| 400 `INCLUDE_UNKNOWN`, `INCLUDE_INVALID` | `include` names a relation this list does not have, or is malformed or repeated. |
</Accordion>

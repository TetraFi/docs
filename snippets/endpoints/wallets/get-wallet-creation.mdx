<Accordion title="Status values">
`pending`, `creating`, `uncertain`, `created`, `active`, `readbackMismatch`, `failed`, `expired`, `cancelled`, `declined`.

- Read it until `status` is `active`. `missing` names who has not approved yet.
- After `failed`, `expired`, `cancelled` or `declined`, start a new wallet.
- The list can grow: read a value you do not know as not usable yet ([Create a wallet](/trade/create-wallet)).

| --- | --- |
| **movementId** | Take it from the account's movements, a window's matches, or a `settlement.payment.matched` webhook. |
| **Owner** | The key's owner also needs View settlements ([Permissions](/authentication#permissions)). |

<Accordion title="Fields to read">
| Field | What it tells you |
| --- | --- |
| `direction`, `amount`, `asset` | What moved into or out of the account. |
| `finalityState` | Whether it is final, including failed, reversed or reorganised transfers. |
| `correlationState` | Whether it is matched, pending a match, or ambiguous. |
| `txHash` | The on-chain transaction, or `null`. |
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Refusals">
| Status and code | Why, and what to do |
| --- | --- |
| 403 `INSUFFICIENT_SCOPE` | The key lacks `settlements_read`. |
| 403 `WORKSPACE_PERMISSION_REQUIRED` | The key's owner lacks View settlements. |
| 404 `VENUE_ACCOUNT_NOT_FOUND` | No venue account with this id in your workspace. |
| 404 `SETTLEMENT_MOVEMENT_NOT_FOUND` | No movement with this id on this account. List them. |
| 503 `SETTLEMENT_MOVEMENTS_ACCOUNT_UNAVAILABLE` | The account could not be read for its movements. Retry shortly. |
</Accordion>

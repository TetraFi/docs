<Accordion title="Fields to read">
| Field | What it tells you |
| --- | --- |
| `activeSelection`, `effectiveSchedule` | The schedule in effect, with its minimum settlement. |
| `termSources` | Where each of its terms comes from. |
| `pendingSelection` | A change waiting on the LP. |
| `nextWindow` | The next window; `nextWindowRolledFrom` when the calendar moved it. |
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Refusals">
| Status and code | Why, and what to do |
| --- | --- |
| 403 `SETTLEMENT_VIEW_PERMISSION_REQUIRED` | The key's owner lacks View settlements. `details.fix` names who can grant it. |
| 403 `INSUFFICIENT_SCOPE` | The key lacks `settlements_read`. |
| 422 `SETTLEMENT_SCHEDULE_UNAVAILABLE` | The LP's published schedule cannot be used. Ask the LP to publish a valid one. |
| 503 `SETTLEMENT_SCHEDULE_READ_UNAVAILABLE` | The schedule could not be read. Retry shortly. |
</Accordion>

Someone with **Manage wallets** creates an organisation hosted wallet in three steps. No wallet exists at the provider until its owners have approved it on the signing page.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Wallet">
    In **Treasury › Accounts**, choose **Add a wallet**, then the hosted wallet and its provider. Pick the connection (**Provided by TetraFi** or your own account), name the wallet and choose its networks.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Owners and approval">
    Add the people who approve its payments and set how many must approve. Each person gets their own invitation and chooses how they approve in their own browser: their own sign-in methods, or one of their own wallets.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review">
    Check the summary and send it. Each owner opens the signing page, sets up how they approve and approves the wallet. It shows as active once the signing page finishes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

No wallet exists at the provider until its owners have approved it on the signing page. Privy offers two connections (provided by TetraFi, or your own app); Turnkey offers one today (your own organisation).

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="In the app">
    Someone with **Manage wallets** creates it in three steps:

    1. **Wallet.** In **Treasury › Accounts**, choose **Add account**, then **Add a wallet**, then the hosted wallet and its provider. Pick the connection, name the wallet and choose its networks.
    2. **Owners and approval.** Add the people who approve its payments and how many must approve. Each person gets their own invitation and chooses how they approve: their own sign-in methods, or one of their own wallets.
    3. **Review.** Send it. Each owner opens the signing page, sets up how they approve and approves. The wallet shows as active once the signing page finishes.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="With the API">
    A key with `treasury_write`, whose member has **Manage wallets**, starts it with [`POST …/wallets`](/trade/create-wallet). Each owner then opens `signingRequest.approvalUrl`; a key never approves.

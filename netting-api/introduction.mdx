[Multilateral Netting](/netting) clears debts by **atomic multilateral set-off** across one network. Integrating means bringing your obligations, signing once, and funding only your net when a run asks.

## Quick Reference

| Facet | |
| --- | --- |
| Protocol | REST over HTTPS, plus push events to your webhook |
| Credential | Workspace API key (`X-API-Key`); `Idempotency-Key` on every write |
| Signature | One EIP-712 envelope per obligation, carried by Permit2 or ERC-3009 |
| On-chain steps | One Permit2 approval per token - you never call a clearing contract |
| Settlement | Lock your net, commit, release only along debts you signed |
| Guarantee | A debt only ever gets smaller; nothing pays out before the run commits |
| Effort | Five touchpoints, the same for every book |

## The Shape of an Integration

| # | Touchpoint | What you do | Surface |
| - | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | [Onboard](/netting-api/integration#onboard) | Hold the membership credentials, set your policy domain | Console |
| 2 | [Submit obligations](/netting-api/integration#submit-obligations) | Bring debts in: cleared quotes, signed debts, or batches | RFQ `mode: cleared`, `POST /clearing/obligations`, `POST /clearing/batches` |
| 3 | [Sign once](/netting-api/integration#sign-once) | One envelope authorizes a lock of your net, inside a window | Permit2 or ERC-3009 |
| 4 | [Each run](/netting-api/integration#each-run) | Read your notice, object or stay silent, your net locks | `notice.published`, `POST /clearing/objections` |
| 5 | [Reconcile](/netting-api/integration#reconcile) | Map your gross book to what moved | `GET /clearing/runs/{id}/statement` |

## Your Own Policy Domain

There is one clearing network, and **modularity** means configuring your participation in it, not running the engine: your own **policy domain**, with your counterparty sets, deadline-tier menu, backstop configuration, funding mode, perimeter policy, and visibility slice. The clearing service is packaged to run on your institution's own infrastructure, or alongside a backend you already operate. Fees: [Monetization](/core-concepts/monetization#fees-on-the-next-layers).

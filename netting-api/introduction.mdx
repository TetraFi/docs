## What Changes in Your Integration

Nothing in the rails flow. Quote, sign, settle stays exactly as the [RFQ API](/rfq-api/introduction) documents it today. Netting arrives as a **second firm quote**: settle instantly at today's price, or clear at a deadline you pick, at a tighter one. Both quotes are firm, the choice is per settlement, and it is the only thing you do differently - whoever created the trade behind it.

On the quote request that means two planned inputs: the settlement mode (instant or cleared) and, for a cleared settlement, the deadline tier - an hours-class tier or T+1 / T+2, a menu rather than one clock. Leave the mode instant and the order moves now, in full, exactly as quoted. Opt it into clearing and one signature records the debt and pre-approves its future collection; nothing leaves your wallet until the pull window opens.

## What a Member Reads and Writes

Everything below is scoped to your own slice. Positions are readable only inside a [sealed engine](/netting/privacy-and-control); you see your own obligations and your own net, never the graph.

| Surface | What it carries |
| --- | --- |
| **Obligations** (read) | Each debt you signed - asset, amount, corridor, counterparty, deadline - with its three numbers: signed, set off, remaining |
| **Net positions** (read) | One signed figure per asset and corridor after each run, the amount you hold balance for |
| **Runs and statements** (read) | Run stage, timing, proof and commit references, and a per-run statement listing every touched obligation, its set-off, and its fee lines |
| **Notices** (read, plus push) | The hold-don't-pay notice per run: keep enough balance for your net; silence accepts |
| **Objections** (write) | A signed objection naming obligation ids, inside the notice window |
| **Resolutions** (write) | Your election when a counterpart is late on a paired leg |

## Funding the Net

After a run, one amount is due at your deadline per asset and corridor. You rank the sources once - wallet balance, a yield position redeemed at collection, an exchange balance withdrawn into the window, a bank rail last - and one atomic pull takes the net from the first source that can pay. Or settle a debt entirely your own way and prove it: both signatures, a custody balance change, or a receipt proven cryptographically closes the obligation on the graph.

## Your Own Policy Domain

There is one clearing network, and **modularity** means configuring your participation in it, not running the engine: your own **policy domain**, with your counterparty sets, deadline-tier menu, backstop configuration, funding mode, perimeter policy, and visibility slice. The clearing service is packaged to run on your institution's own infrastructure, or alongside a backend you already operate.

The engine is domain-blind. TetraFi's own rails are the first obligation source, not the only one: a book that settles gross today - loans on a lending market, receivables, trade finance, payment obligations - enters through an **intake lane** with no engine changes, under the same [compliance policy](/core-concepts/compliance-policy) as everything else. See [Where It Applies](/netting/where-it-applies) for the lanes.

## What Publishes at Launch

* The member surfaces above, with paths, schemas, and the push channel for notices
* The quote-request fields for settlement mode and deadline tier
* Run cadence, deadline tiers, and the backstop ladder's published discounts
* The fee schedule - a small flat rate plus a share of what you saved, measured against your own instant quote
* Membership criteria and the clearing contract addresses

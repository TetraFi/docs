---
title: "Trade by ID"
description: "Fetch one trade's complete dossier, keyed by its order id: trade details, on-chain settlement transactions, LP info, and admin-only compliance sections, scoped to your workspace."
openapi: "GET /workspaces/{id}/trades/{orderId}"
mode: "wide"
---

Trades are keyed by **order id**, not transaction hash - the settlement transaction hashes appear within the trade detail's `settlement` section.

## OpenAPI


Answers to the questions teams ask most on arrival. For anything else, [ask the team](/support).

## Platform

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What is live today, and what is coming?">
    Basic execution is live through [the TetraFi API](/overview):

    - [quotes](/search/how-it-works) from every venue your workspace can use;
    - [orders](/trade/orders) that TetraFi executes on your exchange and OTC venue accounts, or from organisation wallets granted to your key;
    - [prices](/search/prices).

    Advanced execution (TetraFi's router and escrow) is not open yet. [Multilateral Netting](/netting) and the [Shared Collateral Network](/collateral) are in design, with integration shapes sketched under [netting](/netting-api/introduction) and [shared collateral](/collateral-api/introduction) so you can design against them early; [propAMM](/propamm/introduction) is also coming soon.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What are the three primitives?">
    Every institutional settlement carries three costs, and TetraFi runs one primitive against each:

| Primitive | Retires | How |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Settlement rails | Principal risk | Atomic delivery-versus-payment from any chain, asset or venue to any other. |
| Multilateral Netting | The gross settlement toll | Obligations clear through atomic multilateral set-off, so only each participant's net residual settles. |
| Shared Collateral Network | Trapped liquidity | Capital committed once and cross-collateralised across every settlement gap. |

    The rails are the pipes; netting and shared collateral are where the capital efficiency comes from. [How TetraFi Works](/how-tetrafi-works) tells the whole story.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do I have to adopt all three?">
    No: use one or all three, and each plugs into the same shared liquidity and settlement network.

    - A workspace can run firm RFQ liquidity and never touch netting.
    - A book that settles gross today can adopt netting without TetraFi's other rails.
    - A solver can use Solver Credit and nothing else.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Custody and security

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Who holds custody?">
    You do. Your assets stay in your own venue accounts and organisation wallets, and TetraFi executes orders there for you, signing for an organisation wallet only inside the grant its owners approved. Advanced execution, not open yet, is described under [Settlement & Smart Contracts](/core-concepts/settlement-smart-contracts).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What does the compliance policy mean for my integration?">
    Quote requests reach only the venues and LPs your workspace can use, with jurisdiction filtering before a request reaches a desk, and wallets are screened before they trade. [Compliance Policy](/core-concepts/compliance-policy) covers where each check runs.
  </Accordion>

</AccordionGroup>

## Keys, wallets and agents

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does an API key let my backend spend from a wallet?">
    Only from organisation wallets granted to it in the app, and only with [venue trading](/authentication#trade-on-your-venue-accounts); the key cannot grant itself one ([Give a key organisation wallets](/before-you-trade#give-a-key-organisation-wallets)). Treasury transfers stay in the app.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can my backend sign orders without holding keys?">
    Yes. TetraFi signs for an organisation wallet granted to your key, inside the caps its owners approved ([Give a key organisation wallets](/before-you-trade#give-a-key-organisation-wallets)). Signing with a wallet whose key only you hold is rolling out ([Sign with your own wallet](/trade/sign-with-your-own-wallet)). No root key or provider token ever reaches TetraFi.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I let an agent run part of my treasury?">
    Yes (rolling out). An [agent](/core-concepts/agents) is a service account with a human sponsor, an expiry and a kill switch, acting inside an envelope of per-asset caps, velocity, destinations, chains and hours. Every transfer it makes is checked at the gateway before any signer is asked.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Integrating the API

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Is there a test environment?">
    The API has one environment, production at `https://api.tetrafi.io`. Quotes cost nothing, so you can build and test everything up to the order; then order the smallest amount a venue takes ([Test before you trade](/testing)).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="My quote expired before I ordered it. What now?">
    Ordering it is refused with `410 QUOTE_EXPIRED`, and nothing trades. [Requote it](/search/firmness-and-lifetime#requote-a-quote) for a new quote from the same venue, or request new quotes.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="My order request timed out. Did it trade?">
    Maybe. Never order again with a new key: read `GET /api/v1/orders/by-quote/{quoteId}`, which says what the quote produced ([Retries and recovery](/trade/orders#retries-and-recovery)).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I know an order is finished?">
    Read it until `terminal` is `true`; its `status` then says how it ended: `settled`, `unfilled`, `cancelled`, `failed`, `refunded` or `refused` ([Order status](/trade/order-status)).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens to my funds when an order fails?">
    `refunded` means your funds were returned. A `failed` order does not by itself prove nothing traded: read its fills and the trade's `settlement.failureReason` ([Trade history](/trade/trade-history#read-one-trade)).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I cancel an order?">
    Only one not yet sent to a venue, or a pay-in still waiting for your funds: `POST /api/v1/orders/{orderId}/cancel` with its own `Idempotency-Key` ([Place an order](/trade/orders)).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How often can I request quotes?">
    One live `POST /api/v1/quotes` every 3 seconds per key. To watch markets, use [prices](/search/prices), never repeated quotes ([Rate limits](/reference/rate-limits)).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Which time zone do times use?">
    UTC, in RFC 3339 (`2026-10-30T00:40:13.162Z`) ([Conventions](/reference/conventions#values)).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is there an SDK?">
    A TypeScript SDK is not published yet. Every page shows cURL, and the quickstart also Node.js and Python, all runnable as they are ([SDK and samples](/sdk)).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why did a venue not quote?">
    Every venue's outcome is in the quote response's `sources`, with a reason when it did not answer ([Request quotes](/search/how-it-works)).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Solvers, netting and collateral

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I already run a solver. What does Solver Credit change?">
    Nothing about your engine. With [Solver Credit](/collateral/solver-credit) (coming soon), the draw funds the fill transaction itself, never your wallet, so you fill on chains you never stocked; repayment is verified in the same transaction and the escrow releases to the vault when the fill is proven. If you would rather not run a solver, the [engine is also offered as a service](/use-cases/market-makers), leaving your desk with price construction only.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I register interest in netting or the collateral network?">
    Use the routes on the [support page](/support): one for netting adopters, one for depositors and curators, and their own for issuers and solvers. Tell us who owes whom and how often, or what capital you would commit and under what mandate, and the team opens a channel to work in.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

Every fill pays the full toll - the bridge, the rebalance, the conversion - even when equal and opposite flow crosses minutes later, and that toll is the floor under every price you quote or get quoted. Netting is designed to settle a day of fills and corridor payments as one net figure per asset and chain. Lane: **Ships with the primitive** for stablecoin trading flow; payment-network aggregation is **Designed**.

| You will get | How it is designed |
| --- | --- |
| A second firm quote, tighter | [Clear at a deadline you pick](/netting/how-it-works#two-ways-to-settle), or settle instantly at today's price |
| Inventory that stays put | Opposite flows between chains and venues net instead of crossing |
| One figure per asset and chain | Each run, on any chain you have configured |

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What changes for you">
    * **A second firm quote, tighter.** Settle instantly at today's price, or clear at a deadline you pick, at a tighter one. Same request, one more choice.
    * **Fragmentation nets out.** Desks carry inventory on every chain and payment networks pre-fund every corridor to stay open. Opposite flows between chains and venues net against each other instead of crossing, so inventory stays where it already sits - no bridging a position to cover a debt, no rebalancing round-trips.
    * **One figure per asset and chain.** A day of fills or corridor payments settles as one net amount per asset and chain each run, on any chain you have configured - settlement decoupled from where the trade executed.
    * **The first lane to ship.** Cross-chain stablecoin obligations are planned as the first lane, with compliance gates at every run entry.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="A day of flow">
    Three firms on three different rails, one day, USDC:

    * A desk on **Base** owes a payment network on **Arbitrum** - **500 USDC** for fills on its corridor
    * The payment network owes a market maker on **an exchange** - **300 USDC** in corridor payouts
    * The market maker owes the desk - **400 USDC** for fills on the other side

    Gross, that is 1,200 USDC across three settlements, each crossing a chain or a venue. Netted, the loop of 300 cancels with **nothing bridged or withdrawn**, and the rest settles along the original debts: the desk pays the payment network 200, the market maker pays the desk 100. **300 moves instead of 1,200.**
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How it enters">
    Fills will enter at quote time: request a **cleared** quote with a deadline tier, and the trade's settlement becomes an obligation. Corridor payments enter as **signed debts**. From there it is the standard [planned integration](/netting-api/integration#submit-obligations).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

Agents pay per call - for data, inference, tools and each other - and the settlement cost can outweigh the payment itself. Agentic Netting is designed to put that flow on the [one clearing network](/netting): **agents create the obligations; the clearing engine nets them**, deterministically, with a proof checked on-chain every run. No agent decides anything. Lane: **Designed**; it opens to design partners first.

| Today | With Agentic Netting |
| --- | --- |
| Every call pays a toll | Spend nets across platforms, so less of it touches a chain |
| Your batching sees only your book | The counterflow on other platforms cancels too |
| Earnings land on whatever chain the payer used | One residual pays out in the asset and on the chain you choose |

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What changes for you">
    * **Net across platforms, not just inside yours.** The counterflow your own batching can't see cancels too, so less of a day's agent spend ever touches a chain.
    * **Keep your stack.** Your x402 facilitator, wallets, and signers stay as they are: batches go in, one residual comes out. No facilitator yet? Run on ours, with netting for payments your payers authorize under a deferred scheme.
    * **See the saving before anything moves.** Send real batches in analysis mode and get back what would have netted, with nothing executed - your number, on your own flow.
    * **Chains net against each other too.** What your agents earn on one chain offsets what they spend on another, and the residual pays out in the asset and on the chain you choose, over any [supported corridor](/supported-chains).
    * **You stay the member.** Your platform is the counterparty of record. Agents act inside [sponsored spending envelopes](/core-concepts/agents) with a kill switch, your end users never enter the graph, and your flow stays [private](/netting/privacy-and-control) - from other platforms and from TetraFi.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="A day of flow">
    Agents settling on three different rails, one day, USDC:

    * Agents on **an exchange** owe agents on **Base** - **100 USDC**
    * Agents on **Base** owe agents on **Arbitrum** - **80 USDC**
    * Agents on **Arbitrum** owe agents on **the exchange** - **60 USDC**

    Gross, that is 240 USDC across three settlements, each a bridge or a venue withdrawal. Netted, the loop of 60 cancels with **nothing crossing a chain or leaving a venue**, and the rest settles along debts that already exist: the exchange's agents pay Base 40, Base pays Arbitrum 20. **60 moves instead of 240.**
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How it enters">

  </Accordion>

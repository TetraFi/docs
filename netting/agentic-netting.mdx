* **Every call pays a toll.** Agents pay per call - for data, inference, tools, and each other - and the settlement cost can outweigh the payment itself.
* **Your batching sees only your book.** The rest of the counterflow sits on other platforms, out of its sight.
* **Earnings land anywhere** - on whatever chain the payer happened to use.

Agentic Netting puts that flow on the [one clearing network](/netting). **Agents create the obligations; the clearing engine nets them** - deterministically, with a proof checked on-chain every run. No agent decides anything.

## What Changes for You

* **Net across platforms, not just inside yours.** The counterflow your own batching can't see cancels too, so less of a day's agent spend ever touches a chain.
* **Keep your stack.** Your x402 facilitator, wallets, and signers stay as they are: batches go in, one residual comes out. No facilitator yet? Run on ours, with netting for payments your payers authorize under a deferred scheme.
* **See the saving before anything moves.** Send real batches in analysis mode and get back what would have netted, with nothing executed - your number, on your own flow.
* **Chains net against each other too.** What your agents earn on one chain offsets what they spend on another, and the residual pays out in the asset and on the chain you choose, over any [supported corridor](/supported-chains).
* **You stay the member.** Your platform is the counterparty of record. Agents act inside [sponsored spending envelopes](/core-concepts/agents) with a kill switch, your end users never enter the graph, and your flow stays [private](/netting/privacy-and-control) - from other platforms and from TetraFi.

## A Day of Flow

Three platforms, one day, USDC on Base:

* Agents on Platform A owe agents on Platform B **100**
* Agents on Platform B owe agents on Platform C **80**
* Agents on Platform C owe agents on Platform A **60**

Gross, that is 240 USDC across three settlements. Netted, the loop of 60 through all three cancels with nothing moved, and the rest settles along debts that already exist: A pays B 40, B pays C 20. **60 moves instead of 240.**

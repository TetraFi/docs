TetraFi propAMM is a **request-for-stream (RFS)** venue, the streaming counterpart to request-for-quote. In an RFQ, a maker commits to a firm price and must hold and hedge it until expiry, and that risk is priced into the spread. In an RFS, makers stream their latest price on-chain and you execute against the freshest one: no quote to hold, no validity window to defend.

* **Tighter pricing** - makers don't charge for being picked off on a stale number
* **Freshest execution** - trades settle at the most recently streamed price
* **Healthier liquidity** - LPs can quote larger sizes consistently
* **Complementary to RFQ** - firm escrow-backed quotes for a locked-in price; RFS for the best current price at size

TetraFi propAMM is planned as a **request-for-stream (RFS)** venue, the streaming counterpart to request-for-quote. In an RFQ, a maker commits to a firm price and must hold and hedge it until expiry, and that risk is priced into the spread. In an RFS, makers will stream their latest price on-chain and you will execute against the freshest one: no quote to hold, no validity window to defend.

| You will get | Why |
| --- | --- |
| **Tighter pricing** | Makers don't charge for being picked off on a stale number |
| **Freshest execution** | Trades settle at the most recently streamed price |
| **Healthier liquidity** | LPs can quote larger sizes consistently |
| **Complementary to RFQ** | Firm quotes for a locked-in price; RFS for the best current price at size |

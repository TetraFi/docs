## Orders survive a crash

- **Store the idempotency key before you send.** Persist `{quoteId, idempotencyKey, exactBody}`, then send `POST /api/v1/orders`; a retry sends the same key and body bytes ([Retries and recovery](/trade/retries-and-recovery)).
- **Recover, never reorder.** A timeout, `5xx` or `outcomeUnknown` does not mean the order failed: replay with the same key, or find the order by its quote.
- **Order again only on a new quote**, and only when the order is shown not to exist or ended without filling.

- **Follow every order to the end.** Read its `statusUrl` until `terminal` is `true`, every 10 to 15 seconds or when a webhook arrives ([Order status](/trade/order-status)).

## Webhooks are handled once

- **Verify the signature** over the raw body before parsing it.
- **Deduplicate on `webhook-id`:** deliveries arrive at least once and out of order.
- **Answer `2xx` within 10 seconds,** then work. Read `statusUrl` for the current state.
- **Watch for a paused endpoint,** resume it and redeliver what it missed. The delivery log does not survive a TetraFi restart ([Webhooks](/trade/webhooks#retries-pauses-and-missed-events)).

## Signing holds up

- **Sync your clock with NTP.** `X-Timestamp` must be within 300 seconds of TetraFi's clock, and a write's at most 10 seconds ahead.
- **Use fresh nonces.** Generate every `X-Nonce` from a cryptographic random source, and sign each send, retries included, with a new nonce and timestamp ([Sign requests](/authentication#sign-requests)).
- **Keep secrets in a secret manager,** never in code or a tracked file.

## Keys can be rotated

- **Pick up a rotated key within one hour:** after **Rotate key**, the old key works for one hour ([Rotate and revoke](/authentication#rotate-and-revoke)).
- **Grant only the permissions a key uses.**
- **Use a [service account](/service-accounts)** for software that should outlive one person's access.
- **Know how to revoke a leaked key** at once.

## Limits are respected

- **Watch markets with prices,** never by polling quotes: each key may send one live quote request every 3 seconds ([Rate limits](/reference/rate-limits)).

<SearchPrices />

- **On any `429`,** wait `Retry-After` seconds and retry once.
- **Requote a held quote** instead of searching again ([Requotes and expiry](/trade/requotes-and-expiry)).

## Parsing tolerates change

- **Ignore response fields you do not recognise.**
- **Treat an unknown enum value as "other",** such as a status, source reason or `next.kind`: keep following `terminal`, show `detail` or `message`, and carry on.
- **Match refusals on `error`, never `message`,** and treat an unknown code by its status class ([Errors and refusals](/trade/errors#codes-you-do-not-know)).
- **Parse amounts as decimals,** never floats ([Conventions](/reference/conventions)).
- **Read the [Changelog](/changelog)** before each release of your integration.

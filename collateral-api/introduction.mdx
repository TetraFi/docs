This is the planned integration shape of the [Shared Collateral Network](/collateral); no surface is open yet. Capital will be committed once, into permissioned [programmable collateral vaults](/collateral/vaults) with identifiable counterparties, and become the balance sheet the network settles against.

| Planned role | What you will do |
| --- | --- |
| **Draw** | Fund a fill, a T+N leg, a redemption or venue execution through an adapter |
| **Deposit** | Commit capital into a vault whose envelope fits your mandate |
| **Curate** | Deploy your own vault and compete on underwriting |

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Three ways in">
    - **Draw.** A solver funding a fill ([Solver Credit](/collateral/solver-credit)), a desk settling a T+N leg today ([Instant Settlement](/collateral/instant-settlement)), an issuer paying redeeming holders before the underlying settles ([Instant Redemption](/collateral/instant-redemption)), a payment network replacing one balance per venue with a single credit layer ([Venue Prefunding](/collateral/venue-prefunding)). Each reaches the vaults through an adapter, never another buffer.
    - **Deposit.** Commit capital into a vault whose envelope fits your mandate; earn the commitment spread on every draw plus the yield inside the obligations it funds.
    - **Curate.** Deploy your own vault and compete on underwriting, as independent curation opens.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The draw is code">
    Every draw is designed as a priced exchange, not a balance moved on trust. An **adapter** encodes what the capital may do and the proof of how it returns, checked on-chain in the transaction that draws it. Capital is encumbered only while a draw is live, and only to that obligation. Custody, routing, and obligation stay three separate contracts.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Solver Credit for a solver">
    Planned to plug into the fill path you already run. A cross-chain fill needs destination-chain capital while a fill proof releases the escrowed source funds; today that is your balance sheet copied onto every chain. With Solver Credit the draw funds the fill transaction itself, never your wallet, repayment is verified in the same transaction, and the escrow releases to the vault when the fill is proven. The planned flow: quote against the network's headroom on the destination chain, draw at award with the fill's target and calldata bound into the signed draw, and submit the funded fill as one transaction. You would fill TetraFi intents on chains you never stocked, backed by your own bond, and adopt nothing else of ours.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Under the same policy">
    Every draw will re-run your deployment's [compliance policy](/core-concepts/compliance-policy): KYB'd counterparties, jurisdiction filters, machine-checkable policy. An instant redemption inherits the issuer's investor-eligibility rules. Every vault names its own regulated operator; TetraFi runs the rails and is the network's first customer.

    Discount and fee schedules per product, with commitment-spread pricing by duration, publish at launch, along with surfaces, parameters, and contract addresses.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

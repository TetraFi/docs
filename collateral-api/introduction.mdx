## Three Ways In

Capital is committed once, into permissioned [programmable vaults](/collateral/vaults) with identifiable counterparties, and becomes the balance sheet the network settles against. Three roles:

* **Draw.** A solver funding a fill ([Solver Credit](/collateral/solver-credit)), a desk settling a T+N leg today ([Instant Settlement](/collateral/instant-settlement)), an issuer paying redeeming holders before the underlying settles ([Instant Redemption](/collateral/instant-redemption)), a payment network replacing one balance per venue with a single credit layer ([Venue Prefunding](/collateral/venue-prefunding)). Each reaches the vaults through an adapter, never another buffer.
* **Deposit.** Commit capital into a vault whose envelope fits your mandate; earn the commitment spread on every draw plus the yield inside the obligations it funds.
* **Curate.** Deploy your own vault and compete on underwriting, as independent curation opens.

## The Draw Is Code

Every draw is a priced exchange, not a balance moved on trust. An **adapter** encodes what the capital may do and the proof of how it returns, checked on-chain in the transaction that draws it. Capital is encumbered only while a draw is live, and only to that obligation. Custody, routing, and obligation stay three separate contracts.

## Solver Credit for an Existing Solver

Plugged into the fill path you already run. A cross-chain fill needs destination-chain capital while a fill proof releases the escrowed source funds; today that is your balance sheet copied onto every chain. With Solver Credit the draw funds the fill transaction itself, never your wallet, repayment is verified in the same transaction, and the escrow releases to the vault when the fill is proven. Quote against the network's headroom on the destination chain, draw at award with the fill's target and calldata bound into the signed draw, submit the funded fill as one transaction. Fill TetraFi intents on chains you never stocked, backed by your own bond, and adopt nothing else of ours.

## Under the Same Policy

Every draw re-runs your deployment's [compliance policy](/core-concepts/compliance-policy): KYB'd counterparties, jurisdiction filters, machine-checkable policy. An instant redemption inherits the issuer's investor-eligibility rules. Every vault names its own regulated operator; TetraFi runs the rails and is the network's first customer.

Discount and fee schedules per product, with commitment-spread pricing by duration, publish at launch.

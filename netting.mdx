Today every trade settles alone and pays the full toll: the bridge, the rebalance, the conversion. Multilateral Netting is designed to clear those debts by **atomic multilateral set-off** on one shared graph, so only each participant's net residual moves. It opens to design partners first; run timing, fee schedules and membership criteria publish at launch.

| You will get | How it is designed |
| --- | --- |
| A tighter quote | A **cleared** quote settles at a deadline you pick, beside today's instant one |
| Capital that keeps working | Untouched until the lock window, then only your net locks |
| The same counterparty | Set-off, not transfer: no clearing house, nothing novated |
| A proven result | Every run ships a validity proof checked on-chain |
| A private book | Readable only inside a sealed engine; you see your own slice |
| A priced worst case | An unpaid remainder takes a priced ladder, never worse than what you pay today |

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The problem: gross settlement">
    * **Every trade settles alone.** Each fill pays the full toll - the bridge, the rebalance, the conversion - even when an equal and opposite flow crosses the network minutes later. That toll is the floor under every spread you're quoted.
    * **Fragmentation multiplies the toll.** Liquidity is split across dozens of chains and venues, and the flow between them runs both ways - USDC bridged from Base to Arbitrum while as much crosses back.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The fix: set-off">
    * **One shared graph.** Every cleared trade leaves an obligation - who pays whom, how much, by when.
    * **Only the residual moves.** Each run, loops cancel outright and chains clear with a single unit of routed liquidity; each participant's **net residual** settles on rails you already use.
    * **Chains and venues net against each other.** Opposite flows between them net instead of crossing, and the rebalancing cost goes with them.
    * **Extinguished by set-off, not transferred.** No clearing house steps in, nothing is novated, and you keep your original counterparties. Netting never expands your counterparty perimeter.
    * **Measured, not projected.** We plan on **40 to 55% of gross** netting out at the start, rising as the network gets denser. Real systems run from about 10% (closed loops alone) to 96-98% (the CLS and NSCC class); every figure we publish will carry its basis.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Two firm quotes">
    Netting arrives as a second quote - the only thing you will do differently:

    * **Instant** - today's price, settled now, in full.
    * **Cleared** - a deadline you pick, at a **tighter price**: with the rebalancing cost gone, clearing is cheaper to provide.
    * **No margin, no pre-funding** - your capital is untouched until the lock window, then only your net locks. Need out early? A vault on the [Shared Collateral Network](/collateral) buys your position at its published discount.
    * **The fee follows the saving** - a small flat rate plus a share of what you saved against your own instant quote, never a toll on gross.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Your policy domain">
    * **A primitive, not a venue.** There is one clearing network - the way there is one Ethereum - and you configure your participation in it: membership rules, run cadence, backstop order, asset scope, under your compliance policy.
    * **Composes or stands alone.** With [firm quotes and routes](/search/how-it-works) on the rails, and [Shared Collateral Network](/collateral) liquidity - or as the cost layer under whatever you already run.
    * **Plugs in without exposing anything.** Your book, your counterparties, and your edge stay yours - from other members and from TetraFi alike.
  </Accordion>

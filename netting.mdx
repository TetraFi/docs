## The Problem: Gross Settlement

* **Every trade settles alone.** Each fill pays the full toll - the bridge, the rebalance, the conversion - even when an equal and opposite flow crosses the network minutes later. That toll is the floor under every spread you're quoted.
* **Fragmentation multiplies the toll.** Liquidity is split across dozens of chains and venues, and the flow between them runs both ways - USDC bridged from Base to Arbitrum while as much crosses back.

## The Fix: Atomic Multilateral Set-Off

* **One shared graph.** Every cleared trade leaves an obligation - who pays whom, how much, by when.
* **Only the residual moves.** Each run, loops cancel outright and chains clear with a single unit of routed liquidity; each participant's **net residual** settles on rails you already use.
* **Chains and venues net against each other.** Opposite flows between them net instead of crossing, and the rebalancing cost goes with them.
* **Extinguished by set-off, not transferred.** No clearing house steps in, nothing is novated, and you keep your original counterparties.
* **Measured, not projected.** We plan on **40 to 55% of gross** netting out at the start, rising as the network gets denser. Real systems run from about 10% (closed loops alone) to 96-98% (the CLS and NSCC class); every figure we publish carries its basis.

## Two Firm Quotes

Netting arrives as a second quote - the only thing you do differently:

* **Instant** - today's price, settled now, in full.
* **Cleared** - a deadline you pick, at a **tighter price**: with the rebalancing cost gone, clearing is cheaper to provide.
* **Your capital keeps working** - untouched until the lock window, then only your net locks. Need out early? A vault on the [Shared Collateral Network](/collateral) buys your position at its published discount.

## One Network, Your Policy Domain

* **A primitive, not a venue.** There is one clearing network - the way there is one Ethereum - and you configure your participation in it: membership rules, run cadence, backstop order, asset scope, under your compliance policy.
* **Composes or stands alone.** With [RFQ](/rfq-api/introduction) firm quotes, [Router](/router-api/introduction) execution, and [Shared Collateral Network](/collateral) liquidity - or as the cost layer under whatever you already run.
* **Plugs in without exposing anything.** Your book, your counterparties, and your edge stay yours - from other members and from TetraFi alike.

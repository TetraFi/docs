Your backend authenticates with an API key. A key belongs to you and to one workspace, and it can never do more than you can do in that workspace: TetraFi checks your current permissions on every request, so if yours shrink, the key's shrink with them. For software that should not depend on one person's account, use a [service account](/service-accounts).

## Create an API key

1. In the app, open **Settings**. Under **Your account**, find **Personal API keys**, select **Open API keys**, then **Create key**.
2. Give the key a **Name**. **Description**, **Expiration** and **IP allowlist** are optional unless your workspace requires keys to expire; a key without an expiration works until you revoke it.
3. Under **Permissions**, tick what the key may do (below). To quote and order, tick **Read quotes**, **Submit orders** and **Read orders**.
4. To trade through your venue accounts, switch **Venue trading** on. You must be a workspace owner or admin who manages credentials, signed in with a second factor.
5. Select **Create key**. Copy **API Key** and, with venue trading, **Request-signing secret** into your secret manager, separately. Both are shown once and cannot be recovered; never put them in source code or a tracked file.

## Permissions

A key can receive only permissions you hold in that workspace.

| Permission in the app | What the key can do |
| --- | --- |
| **Read quotes** | List assets and read prices. With **Submit orders**, request quotes: **Read quotes** alone cannot. |
| **Submit orders** | Place and cancel orders, and, with **Read quotes**, request quotes. |
| **Read orders** | Read an order's status and recover an order by its quote. |
| **Read trades** | Read the trade history. |
| **Treasury read** | Read [balances](/trade/balances), exchange accounts and credit. Never moves money. |
| **Read settlements** | Read [settlement](/trade/settlement-and-credit) plans, windows, obligations and movements. |
| **Read fee schedule** | Read your [fee schedule](/trade/fee-schedule). No other permission includes it. |
| **Read compliance** | Read your workspace's [capabilities](/before-you-trade#check-readiness-from-your-backend). |
| **Manage webhooks** | Create and manage [webhooks](/trade/webhooks). |

The [permission matrix](/reference/permissions) maps every operation to its scope.

## Trade on your venue accounts

Every quote you can order today is Basic: TetraFi executes it from one of your exchange or OTC venue accounts, or from an organisation wallet granted to your key. A key that trades Basic quotes needs:

- **Venue trading**, given only when the key is created, together with **Read quotes** and **Submit orders**, by its owner signed in with a second factor. It cannot be added to an existing key.
- **Request signing**, always on for a venue-trading key: every request it sends must be [signed](#sign-every-request). It cannot be turned off (`409 VENUE_TRADE_KEY_SIGNING_REQUIRED`). For unsigned requests, use a key without venue trading.
- **Organisation wallets granted to it**, to trade from them. **Create API key** grants your default organisation wallets that you hold a Trade right on; change them on the key's **Wallets** tab ([Give a key organisation wallets](/before-you-trade#give-a-key-organisation-wallets)). Venue accounts need no grant.

Without venue trading, a quote request still answers `200`, but returns no quotes from your venue accounts: they read `restricted` with `venueTradeScopeRequired`.

### Check what a key can do

`GET /api/v1/me` has no side effects and describes the key that called it, not a user profile:

| Field | What it says |
| --- | --- |
| `authKind`, `auth` | `apiKey`; `auth` is `oauthClientCredentials` for a [service-account token](/service-accounts). |
| `keyId`, `name`, `origin` | The key's identity. `keyId` is also its `credentialId`. |
| `scopes`, `permissions`, `dataScope` | Its current authority. |
| `workspaceId` | The workspace the key is bound to. |
| `organisationWallets` | Its venue trading and wallet grants: `venueTradeScope` and `venueTradeEnabled` must both be `true` for venue-account quotes ([Check readiness](/before-you-trade#check-readiness-from-your-backend)). |
| `rateLimits`, `expiresAt` | The key's own request limit and expiry, when set. |

A signing key must sign this call too.

## Send the key

Send `X-API-Key: <your key>` on every request. The key is bound to its workspace, so `X-Workspace-Id` is optional; when you send it, it must name the key's own workspace (`403 WORKSPACE_SCOPE_MISMATCH` otherwise, `400 INVALID_WORKSPACE_ID` when malformed). A signed-in member's session has no workspace of its own and must always send it (`400 MISSING_WORKSPACE`).

Every path under `/api/v1/workspaces/{workspaceId}/`, such as balances, trades, settlement, venue accounts, the fee schedule, capabilities and webhooks, names the workspace: use the `workspaceId` from `GET /api/v1/me`. Quotes and orders do not. A request without credentials answers `401 AUTH_REQUIRED` before anything is looked up.

## Sign every request

When signing is on, every request carries three more headers:

| Header | Value |
| --- | --- |
| `X-Timestamp` | The current Unix time in seconds: within 300 seconds of TetraFi's clock, and a write at most 10 seconds ahead. Keep your clock NTP-synced. |
| `X-Nonce` | On a write (`POST`, `PUT`, `PATCH`, `DELETE`) only: a fresh random value, 16 to 128 characters of `A-Z`, `a-z`, `0-9`, `-` and `_`. |
| `X-Signature` | The lower-case hexadecimal HMAC-SHA256 of the lines below, keyed with the signing secret's 32 bytes. The secret is 64 hexadecimal characters: decode it first. |

A write signs five lines joined by newlines, with no trailing newline; a read leaves out the nonce line:

```text
<X-Timestamp>
<X-Nonce>
<HTTP method in upper case>
<path with its query string, exactly as sent>
<lower-case hexadecimal SHA-256 of the exact body bytes>
```

A request without a body hashes zero bytes. Generate the nonce from a cryptographic source, for example 18 random bytes in base64url:

```bash
nonce=$(openssl rand -base64 18 | tr '+/' '-_' | tr -d '=')
```

In Node.js, `crypto.randomBytes(18).toString('base64url')`; in Python, `secrets.token_urlsafe(18)`.

Each nonce is accepted once per key, by every TetraFi server, restarts included. So a retry of a write signs again with a new nonce and timestamp while its `Idempotency-Key` and body stay the same ([Retries and recovery](/trade/retries-and-recovery)). A read sends no nonce, so the same signed read is accepted again within the 300-second window, and polling needs no nonce.

A failed check answers `401 AUTH_REQUIRED` with `details.reason`:

| `details.reason` | What went wrong |
| --- | --- |
| `SIGNATURE_REQUIRED` | The key needs signing and a header is missing. |
| `SIGNATURE_INVALID` | The signature is malformed or does not match. |
| `TIMESTAMP_OUT_OF_RANGE` | `X-Timestamp` is more than 300 seconds off, or a write's is more than 10 seconds ahead. |
| `NONCE_REQUIRED`, `NONCE_INVALID` | A write has no `X-Nonce`, or it is malformed or sent twice. |
| `SIGNATURE_REPLAYED` | This nonce was already accepted for the key. |

The rate limit is checked first: a replay sent while the key is rate limited answers `429`. `503 API_KEY_UNAVAILABLE` means the key or its signing state could not be confirmed; the nonce may have been recorded, so sign again with a new timestamp and nonce before you retry. The [quickstart](/quickstart) script signs every request when `TETRAFI_SIGNING_SECRET` is set.

### Rotate the signing secret

Rotating or enabling signing is a credential operation of the app's signed-in session, not something your backend does with its key: `POST /api/v1/workspaces/{workspaceId}/credentials/{credentialId}/signing`, where `credentialId` is the key's `keyId`, answers a new `signingSecret` once and replaces the old one at once. An API key or service-account token is refused with `403 JWT_REQUIRED`, and only the key's owner (or, for a service-account key, a member who manages credentials) may call it.

## Rotate and revoke

- **Rotate.** Choose **Rotate key** on the key. A new key is issued, and the old one keeps working for one hour so you can deploy the new one. The rotated key keeps its permissions and its signing secret.
- **Revoke.** Choose **Revoke key**. Revoking is permanent: the key is refused within seconds, at most 30 seconds later, with `401 AUTH_REQUIRED` and `details.reason` `INVALID_API_KEY`.

A key past its expiration answers `401 AUTH_REQUIRED` with `API_KEY_EXPIRED`. Quote and other rate limits are on [Rate limits](/reference/rate-limits).

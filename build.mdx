## What you're building on

Every institutional settlement carries three costs: **principal risk**, the **gross settlement** toll, and **trapped liquidity**. TetraFi runs one primitive against each, on the same network and under one **compliance policy**.

| Primitive | The cost it removes | Status | Where you integrate |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Settlement rails** | Principal risk | Basic execution is live. Escrow-settled delivery-versus-payment (Advanced) is not open yet. | [The TetraFi API](/overview): [quotes](/search/how-it-works), [orders](/trade/orders), [prices](/search/prices) |
| **[Multilateral Netting](/netting)** | Gross settlement: obligations clear by atomic multilateral set-off, and only the net residual settles | Coming soon | [Integration](/netting-api/integration) |
| **[Shared Collateral Network](/collateral)** | Trapped liquidity: capital committed once, cross-collateralised across every settlement gap | Coming soon | [Integration shape](/collateral-api/introduction) |

## What is live: Basic execution

* **One request, every venue.** A quote request asks every venue your workspace can use and returns comparable quotes, with every venue's outcome.
* **Orders on your own accounts.** TetraFi executes the order you place on your connected exchange and OTC venue accounts, or from organisation wallets granted to your key. Your assets stay in your accounts.
* **What you integrate:** quote, then order, then follow its status or [webhooks](/trade/webhooks).

With Advanced execution, not open yet, an order settles through your own immutable [router and escrow](/core-concepts/settlement-smart-contracts): funds release against proof of delivery or refund through a path nobody can pause.

## Coming soon

**Multilateral Netting.** Your trade price is never touched: a run only decides who pays whom how much, with a validity proof checked on-chain first. You integrate [five touchpoints](/netting-api/integration): onboard, submit obligations, sign once, act on each run's notice, reconcile the statement. Agent platforms add [batches from their own stack](/netting-api/agentic-netting).

**Shared Collateral Network.** A draw is a priced exchange verified in the transaction that makes it, funding the fill or settlement leg itself and encumbered only while live. You draw, deposit or curate.

## What the platform handles for you

Who may trade, from which account or wallet, under whose approval: the workspace runs it under one compliance policy. A key acts only within its owner's live permissions, and trades from an organisation wallet only when that wallet is granted to it. Full list and status: [Platform capabilities](/platform).

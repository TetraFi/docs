## What You're Building On

Every institutional settlement carries three costs: the **principal risk** of releasing one leg before the other lands, the **gross settlement** toll of moving full notional when only the net is owed, and the **trapped liquidity** parked to cover the wait. TetraFi runs one **primitive** against each. The three are **composable** - use one or all three, and each plugs into the same shared liquidity and settlement network - and every deployment is **modular**, under **full institutional control**: your own immutable on-chain router with no custody key and no upgrade key, the venues it may touch, which LPs and jurisdictions your requests reach, and how your compliance pipeline is composed. One **compliance policy** gates every layer. Own the deployment, share the network.

| Primitive | The cost it removes | Status | Where you integrate |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Settlement rails** | Principal risk - atomic delivery-versus-payment from any chain, asset, or venue to any other | Live | [RFQ API](/rfq-api/introduction), [Router API](/router-api/introduction), [Price API](/price-api/introduction), [Trade History API](/trade-history-api/introduction) |
| **[Multilateral Netting](/netting)** | Gross settlement - obligations clear by **atomic multilateral set-off**, and only the net residual settles | Coming soon | [Integration shape](/netting-api/introduction) |
| **[Shared Collateral Network](/collateral)** | Trapped liquidity - committed once, cross-collateralised across every settlement gap | Coming soon | [Integration shape](/collateral-api/introduction) |

## Each Primitive, One Level Deeper

**Settlement rails**

* **The invariant:** funds move only on a signed order's terms, through your own immutable router, into an escrow that releases against proof of delivery or refunds through a path nobody can pause.
* **What you integrate:** quote, preflight, sign, submit. The preflight plan is the whole contract.
* **What never changes:** custody never leaves your side; a capability upgrade only ever adds a venue module.

**Multilateral Netting** (coming soon)

* **The invariant:** your trade price is never touched. A run only decides who pays whom how much, by atomic multilateral set-off, and a validity proof is checked on-chain before anything executes.
* **What you integrate:** one extra choice per settlement - instant, or clear at a deadline you pick, at a tighter price - plus a member surface for your own obligations, positions, runs, and notices.
* **What never changes:** the residual settles on the rails above, in your own policy domain, readable only inside your own slice.

**Shared Collateral Network** (coming soon)

* **The invariant:** a draw is a priced exchange verified in the transaction that makes it, funding the fill or the settlement leg itself, never a wallet. Capital is encumbered only while a draw is live, and only to that one obligation.
* **What you integrate:** draw, deposit, or curate.
* **What never changes:** every draw re-runs your compliance policy; custody, routing, and obligation stay separate.

The rails are the pipes. Netting and shared collateral are where the **capital efficiency** comes from, and every layer plugs into the same escrow and the same policy: see [Settlement & Smart Contracts](/core-concepts/settlement-smart-contracts) and [Compliance Policy](/core-concepts/compliance-policy). The rest of this page is the live surface.

### Picking Your Product

|                    | **RFQ API - Firm Escrow Liquidity**                                  | **Router API - Aggregated Multi-Source**                                                   |
| ------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **What you buy**   | Only firm quotes, each escrow-enforced to deliver or refund         | A ranked candidate list, each entry carrying its own stated guarantees                     |
| **How it settles** | Delivery-versus-payment through escrow, custody never with TetraFi     | On-chain through the same contract stack, custody never with TetraFi                         |
| **Reach**          | The corridors your workspace's solvers and LPs actively quote        | Everything eligible - DEX, bridges, issuers, fiat rails, cross-chain corridors             |
| **The catch**      | Coverage follows the LP roster, not the whole token universe         | Guarantees differ candidate to candidate - always read the printed terms                   |

**RFQ is your product if:**

* Escrow-enforced delivery-or-refund is the guarantee your users depend on
* Your flow concentrates in quoted corridors, at real size
* Settlement predictability outranks maximum coverage

**Router is your product if:**

* Coverage is king - DEX, bridge, issuer, and fiat sources all in play
* You'd rather compare ranked candidates than accept a single answer
* Speed, price, and trust are per-trade decisions in your pipeline

**Run both if:**

* Core corridors deserve firm escrow while the long tail rides the router
* No single source outage should ever block an executable trade

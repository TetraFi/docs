## What You're Building On

Every institutional settlement carries three costs: **principal risk**, the **gross settlement** toll, and **trapped liquidity**. TetraFi runs one **primitive** against each. The three are **composable** - use one or all, each on the same shared liquidity and settlement network - and every deployment is **modular**, under **full institutional control**: your own immutable on-chain router, the venues, LPs, and jurisdictions it reaches, and how your compliance pipeline is composed. One **compliance policy** gates every layer. Own the deployment, share the network.

| Primitive | The cost it removes | Status | Where you integrate |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Settlement rails** | Principal risk - atomic delivery-versus-payment from any chain, asset, or venue to any other | Live | [The TetraFi API](/overview): [quotes](/search/how-it-works), [orders](/trade/orders), [prices](/search/prices) |
| **[Multilateral Netting](/netting)** | Gross settlement - obligations clear by **atomic multilateral set-off**, and only the net residual settles | Coming soon | [Integration](/netting-api/integration) |
| **[Shared Collateral Network](/collateral)** | Trapped liquidity - committed once, cross-collateralised across every settlement gap | Coming soon | [Integration shape](/collateral-api/introduction) |

## Each Primitive, One Level Deeper

**Settlement rails**

* **The invariant:** funds move only on a signed order's terms, through your own immutable router, into an escrow that releases against proof of delivery or refunds through a path nobody can pause.
* **What you integrate:** quote, preflight, sign, submit. The preflight plan is the whole contract.
* **What never changes:** custody stays on your side; an upgrade only ever adds a venue module.

**Multilateral Netting** (coming soon)

* **The invariant:** your trade price is never touched. A run only decides who pays whom how much, by atomic multilateral set-off, with a validity proof checked on-chain first.
* **What you integrate:** [five touchpoints](/netting-api/integration) - onboard, submit obligations (a cleared quote, a signed debt, or a batch), sign once, act on each run's notice, reconcile the statement. Agent platforms add [batches from their own stack](/netting-api/agentic-netting).
* **What never changes:** the residual settles on the rails, in your own policy domain, readable only inside your own slice.

**Shared Collateral Network** (coming soon)

* **The invariant:** a draw is a priced exchange verified in the transaction that makes it, funding the fill or settlement leg itself, never a wallet, and encumbered only while live, only to that obligation.
* **What you integrate:** draw, deposit, or curate.
* **What never changes:** every draw re-runs your compliance policy; custody, routing, and obligation stay separate.

The rails are the pipes; netting and shared collateral are where the **capital efficiency** comes from. Every layer plugs into the same [escrow](/core-concepts/settlement-smart-contracts) and [policy](/core-concepts/compliance-policy).

### One API, every venue

You send one quote request and get back a quote from every venue and route your workspace can reach: firm quotes from solvers and LPs, and routes through DEXs, bridges, and other sources. Each quote states its own terms - whether it is `indicative` or `executable` and firm, the `assurance` behind it, and every fee line - so always read the printed terms before you order. Settlement runs through the same contract stack either way, and custody never sits with TetraFi. Still unsure? [Get in touch](/support).

Around the trade: [prices](/search/prices) give you an indicative per-venue picture for a pair before you ask for quotes, and your workspace's trade history is filterable and exportable.

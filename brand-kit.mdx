TetraFi uses **institutional mono**: the original modular symbol and a compact, mixed-case **TetraFi** wordmark in one ink. Identity stays quiet so actions and financial states remain clear.

## The mark

- Four modules on the original 32-unit canvas: keep proportions, spacing and the small corner radius.
- Use the supplied artwork, never a letter T, a library icon or a redrawn approximation.
- Source: `tetrafi-ui/src/shared/ui/icons/tetra-mark.tsx`. The open mark inherits the surrounding primary ink, with no tile behind it.
- The contained favicon and app icon are separate exports in `tetrafi-ui/src/public/`.

## The wordmark

- Write **TetraFi**, with a capital T and F.
- The compact lockup uses the application's Geist sans face, semibold weight and section-role tracking.
- Keep the word together: neither “Fi” nor any individual module receives a separate colour.
- Use the full lockup where there is room; the symbol alone only where the name is evident or space is tight, such as a browser tab or app launcher.
- Do not present live text as an approved outlined vector master.

## Monochrome in both themes

- On a dark canvas, use the reversed mark and wordmark in primary light ink.
- On a light canvas, use primary dark ink for both parts.
- The contained app icon uses a white symbol on a near-black rounded square in either theme.
- Reserve cobalt for actions, links and selection. Green, amber and red communicate operational states, not alternate brand palettes.
- The former cyan-on-navy tile, split-colour uppercase lockup and green-to-cyan brand gradient are retired treatments.

Do not add a gradient, glow, outline, shadow or independent colour inside the mark. Use a quiet, high-contrast background rather than placing it over photography or changing financial data.

## Clear space

Let **M** be the height of the symbol's square canvas, excluding any external container.

| Use | Minimum clear space on every side |
| --- | --- |
| Compact application lockup | M / 2 |
| External communications or co-branding | M |

- Keep text, controls, borders and partner logos out of it.
- The gap between symbol and wordmark is part of the lockup, not extra clear space.
- Do not squeeze the symbol or letter spacing to fit a narrow placement; use the symbol-only form instead.

## Minimum sizes

| Asset | Minimum size |
| --- | --- |
| Open symbol | 24px square |
| Full external lockup | 120px wide, with a symbol at least 24px square |
| Compact application signature | 24px symbol with the existing readable label role |
| Favicon | Dedicated 16px, 32px and 48px exports |
| Touch icon | Dedicated 180px export |
| App launcher | Dedicated 192px and 512px exports |

Use the dedicated contained icon at favicon sizes; never shrink a complete lockup to 16px. Inspect exports at their actual display size, not only enlarged.

## Assets and accessibility

- The application's public asset directory serves `favicon.svg`, `app-icon.svg`, raster favicons, the touch icon and launcher icons.
- `site.webmanifest` names the launcher exports; the document head names the favicon and touch asset.
- The live system gallery's **Brand** specimen shows the same application owners, not recreated artwork.
- A mark beside the visible wordmark is decorative: hide that SVG from assistive technology.
- Name a standalone linked signature **TetraFi home**; the link owns the accessible name and keyboard focus.
- Never imply verification, custody or settlement success through the brand mark.
- Real venue, asset and chain identities stay in their registry-keyed logo system. Do not recolour or generate a known partner's mark to resemble TetraFi.

## What not to do

- Do not stretch, rotate, skew, rearrange or round the modules differently.
- Do not write TETRAFI, Tetrafi or Tetra FI in a new lockup.
- Do not colour “Fi” separately or reuse the retired cyan treatment.
- Do not put the open mark in a decorative tile; use the contained app icon only for its launcher role.
- Do not animate the identity, attach status badges to it or substitute a financial status colour.
- Do not use documentation starter logos as TetraFi master artwork.

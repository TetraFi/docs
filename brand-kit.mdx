TetraFi uses **institutional mono**: the original modular symbol and compact, mixed-case **TetraFi** wordmark in one ink. Identity stays quiet so actions and financial states remain clear.

## The mark

The symbol has four modules on its original 32-unit canvas. Keep their proportions, spacing and small corner radius unchanged. Use the supplied artwork, not a letter T, a library icon or a newly drawn approximation.

The application source is `tetrafi-ui/src/shared/ui/icons/tetra-mark.tsx`. The open mark inherits the surrounding primary ink and has no tile behind it. The contained favicon and app icon are separate exports in `tetrafi-ui/src/public/`.

## The wordmark

Write **TetraFi**, with a capital T and F. The compact lockup uses the application's Geist sans face, semibold weight and section-role tracking. Keep the word together: neither “Fi” nor any individual module receives a separate colour.

Use the full lockup where there is room. Use the symbol alone only when the product name is already evident or the surface is constrained, such as a browser tab or app launcher. Do not present live text as an approved outlined vector master.

## Monochrome in both themes

- On a dark canvas, use the reversed mark and wordmark in primary light ink.
- On a light canvas, use primary dark ink for both parts.
- The contained app icon uses a white symbol on a near-black rounded square in either theme.
- Reserve cobalt for actions, links and selection. Green, amber and red communicate operational states, not alternate brand palettes.
- The former cyan-on-navy tile, split-colour uppercase lockup and green-to-cyan brand gradient are retired treatments.

Do not add a gradient, glow, outline, shadow or independent colour inside the mark. Use a quiet, high-contrast background rather than placing it over photography or changing financial data.

## Clear space

Let **M** be the height of the symbol's square canvas, excluding any external container.

| Use | Minimum clear space on every side |
| --- | --- |
| Compact application lockup | M / 2 |
| External communications or co-branding | M |

Keep text, controls, borders and partner logos outside that space. The gap between symbol and wordmark is part of the lockup, not extra clear space. Do not squeeze the symbol or letter spacing to make a narrow placement fit; use the symbol-only form instead.

## Minimum sizes

| Asset | Minimum size |
| --- | --- |
| Open symbol | 24px square |
| Full external lockup | 120px wide, with a symbol at least 24px square |
| Compact application signature | 24px symbol with the existing readable label role |
| Favicon | Dedicated 16px, 32px and 48px exports |
| Touch icon | Dedicated 180px export |
| App launcher | Dedicated 192px and 512px exports |

Use the dedicated contained icon at favicon sizes; do not shrink a complete lockup to 16px. Inspect exports at their actual display size, not only enlarged.

## Assets and accessibility

The application serves `favicon.svg`, `app-icon.svg`, raster favicon sizes, the touch icon and the launcher icons from its public asset directory. `site.webmanifest` names the launcher exports; the document head names the favicon and touch asset. The live system gallery's **Brand** specimen shows the same application owners, not recreated artwork.

A mark beside the visible wordmark is decorative. Hide that SVG from assistive technology. Name a standalone linked signature **TetraFi home**; the link owns the accessible name and keyboard focus. Never imply verification, custody or settlement success through the brand mark.

Real venue, asset and chain identities remain in their registry-keyed logo system. Do not recolour or generate a known partner's mark to make it resemble TetraFi.

## What not to do

- Do not stretch, rotate, skew, rearrange or round the modules differently.
- Do not write TETRAFI, Tetrafi or Tetra FI in a new lockup.
- Do not colour “Fi” separately or reuse the retired cyan treatment.
- Do not put the open mark in a decorative tile; use the contained app icon only for its launcher role.
- Do not animate the identity, attach status badges to it or substitute a financial status colour.
- Do not use documentation starter logos as TetraFi master artwork.
